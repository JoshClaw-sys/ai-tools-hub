Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency matters. AI Tools Hub participates in affiliate programs with various AI tool vendors. This page explains exactly how that works.

What is an affiliate link?

When you click "Get started" or "Try free" on a tool page and sign up or make a purchase, we may receive a small commission (typically $5-50 per signup depending on the tool). The price you pay is the same — there's no extra cost to you.

Does this bias our recommendations?

No. Our editorial process is the same whether or not a product is in an affiliate program. We:

  • Recommend products we'd genuinely use ourselves
  • Mark products we recommend skipping with a clear "Skip" badge
  • Update recommendations every 60-90 days based on new tools and pricing
  • Never accept payment for placement or positive coverage

Which programs do we participate in?

We currently participate in affiliate programs for OpenAI, Anthropic, Midjourney, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and other major AI tools. This list changes as tools launch new programs.

Questions?

If you have questions about our affiliate relationships or editorial process, contact us.
