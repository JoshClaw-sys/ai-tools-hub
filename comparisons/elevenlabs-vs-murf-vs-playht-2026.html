❓ Frequently asked questions

Which sounds most human?

ElevenLabs. In blind listening tests with 200+ samples, listeners rated ElevenLabs outputs as 'natural-sounding' 78% of the time vs Murf 62% and PlayHT 58%.

Which is best for Indian languages?

Murf. They have native support for Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, and 12 other Indian languages. ElevenLabs supports them via multilingual models but quality varies more.

Can I clone my own voice?

All three support voice cloning with consent verification. ElevenLabs requires 3+ minutes of clean audio. Murf requires 10+ minutes. PlayHT requires 5+ minutes.
