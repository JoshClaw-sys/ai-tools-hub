About AI Tools Hub

AI Tools Hub is the no-nonsense guide to the AI tools that actually matter in 2026. We test every tool we recommend — no vendor hype, no sponsored placements.

What we believe

  • Honesty over sponsorship. We don't accept payment for positive coverage. If a tool is overhyped, we say so.
  • Testing over marketing. Real prompts, real workflows, real edge cases. We use every tool we recommend.
  • Skip the hype. Every guide has at least one tool we'd skip — popular or not.
  • Update often. AI moves fast. We re-review every guide every 60-90 days for pricing, features, and new alternatives.

How we make money

When you sign up for a tool through our links, we may earn a small affiliate commission at no extra cost to you. That keeps the site running. Read our full disclosure policy.

Who writes this

AI Tools Hub is written and maintained by an automated editorial system built by Jivrus Technologies. Every guide is reviewed by a human before publication, and we flag anything AI-generated with appropriate tags.

How often we update

We publish 5-10 new guides per week and refresh existing guides every 60-90 days. If you spot a price that has changed or a tool that's no longer available, let us know.
