## Quick verdict &mdash; our top pick

For source-grounded research summarization with the strongest free tier, **NotebookLM** is the top pick. The free tier supports 50 notebooks with 100 sources each and generates unlimited Audio Overview podcasts from any notebook. Answers cite the exact passages in your uploaded documents - it does not pull from the open web - which makes it the safest summarizer for academic and policy work. Premium tiers (Google AI Plus at $9.99/mo, AI Pro at $19.99/mo) raise the limits and unlock full multi-host Audio Overviews. For cross-source summarization (PDF + web URL + pasted text), ChatGPT Plus at $20/mo is the strongest versatile pick.
