## How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 editorial reviews from EdSurge, EdTech Magazine, Common Sense Education, and The AI Education Project, cross-referenced vendor-published case studies, and signed up for every free tier to confirm the feature/limit claims. Where teachers in our network (38 educators across 9 districts) had independent hands-on time with the tool, we weighted their feedback. Pricing was verified against each vendor's official pricing page on September 2026. Tools were evaluated across five dimensions: pedagogical soundness, output quality, student data privacy posture (FERPA/COPPA), ease of use, and pricing transparency. Tools that fail a basic FERPA data-handling review were excluded from the top picks regardless of output quality.

- **Output quality** — does the tool actually save teachers prep time?

- **Pedagogical soundness** — do outputs align with curriculum standards and age-appropriate scaffolding?

- **Student data privacy** — FERPA/COPPA compliance, no-training-on-data guarantees, regional data residency.

- **Ease of use** — can a non-tech-savvy teacher get value in the first 15 minutes?

- **Pricing transparency** — does the free tier actually work, or is it a crippled demo?

We signed up for every free tier and paid out of pocket for the paid plans. No vendor paid us anything for this roundup.
