## How we evaluated these tools

We compared eight leading AI data visualization tools across five dimensions: chart-generation quality (does a natural-language prompt produce a chart that is actually useful, or just pretty?), data-source support (CSV, Excel, Google Sheets, Postgres, MySQL, Snowflake, BigQuery), natural-language query accuracy (can the tool generate correct SQL or DAX from a sentence?), dashboard polish (does the output look like something you would actually share?), and free-tier usefulness (can a single analyst or a small team run real analysis without paying). Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in September 2026.

Roughly 60 prompts were used to compare each tool's first-draft output on the same brief: a sales-by-region dataset, a time-series of monthly active users, and a cohort-style retention table. We measured time-to-first-chart, the number of follow-up prompts required to reach a shareable state, and whether the free tier was usable for the full workflow or stopped at a paywall halfway through. Each candidate tool was also evaluated for collaboration features (comments, share links, scheduled refresh), export fidelity (PNG, PDF, PPTX), and how well it handles dense tables versus narrative-style summaries. Public benchmarks from G2, Capterra, and the Tableau and Power BI community blogs were used as a sanity check on the rankings.
