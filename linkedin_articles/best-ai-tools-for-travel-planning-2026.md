## Quick verdict — our top picks

After evaluating the leading AI travel planning tools in 2026, the right pick depends on how you actually plan trips. For most travelers, **Mindtrip** is the best all-around AI travel planner — chat-based with shared itineraries, map integration, and a clean free tier. For WhatsApp-first travelers, **Layla** (formerly BeautifulTrip) is the right pick. For backpackers who want detailed hour-by-hour itineraries, **Wonderplan** is the strongest free option.

The category has matured significantly since 2023 when Roam Around went viral as a "ChatGPT wrapper for travel." Today's leading tools — Mindtrip, Layla, Wonderplan, and Roam Around — each win at one thing: **Mindtrip** on save-and-share collaboration, **Layla** on multi-channel reach, **Wonderplan** on itinerary detail, and **Roam Around** on mobile-first city curation.

**Best for most people:** Mindtrip — chat-based, shareable, free.
**Best for WhatsApp-first travelers:** Layla.
**Best for backpackers:** Wonderplan.
**Best for mobile-first city trips:** Roam Around.

## How we picked (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 reviews from youngju.dev's category deep-dive, product reviews on G2 and Product Hunt, and direct vendor documentation. We compared the four leading dedicated AI travel planners on five dimensions: itinerary detail, collaboration features, multi-channel support, free vs paid tier usefulness, and booking integration depth.

We excluded general LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) from the dedicated category ranking because they cannot book flights, share editable itineraries with groups, or integrate with maps the way a dedicated travel planner can. For a one-off "plan me a 5-night Bali itinerary" prompt, ChatGPT is still a fine first draft — but for ongoing trip planning with collaboration, the dedicated tools win.

Pricing was verified against each vendor's public pricing page on September 21,2026. Free tier usefulness was assessed based on whether the free tier can plan a real trip (not a crippled preview). This is not a hands-on benchmark — try the free tiers and validate against your own travel style before committing.
