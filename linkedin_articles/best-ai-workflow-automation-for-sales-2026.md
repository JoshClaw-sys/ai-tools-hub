## Clay

  

Free 100 credits, Starter $149/mo, Pro $349/mo, Enterprise custom
  

**Best for:** Outbound sales, AI-enriched lead lists, multi-step personalized sequences
  
    
- Widest data enrichment coverage of any sales tool in 2026 (100+ providers)
    
- Claygent adds AI research inside the workflow
    
- Conditional multi-channel sequences built in

## Zapier

  

Free 100 tasks, Starter $19.99/mo, Professional $49/mo, Team $69/mo, Enterprise custom
  

**Best for:** Cross-app workflows, non-technical teams, the broadest integration library
  
    
- Largest app integration library in the category (6,000+)
    
- Lowest learning curve for non-technical users
    
- Tables, Interfaces, and Chatbots added in 2026
    
- SOC 2 Type II + HIPAA compliance available
  
  
    
- Per-task pricing gets expensive at scale
    
- Weaker native data enrichment than Clay
    
- Multi-step branching is more limited than Make
