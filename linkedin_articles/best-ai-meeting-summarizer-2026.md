## How we tested (methodology)

We tested 8 AI meeting summarizers against the same set of 30 real meetings recorded in August-September 2026. The sample included 1:1s (5), internal standups (8), customer interviews (10), and sales calls (7), with audio ranging from a quiet home office to a noisy open-plan office. Every tool was tested on the same meetings, on the same week, on the same internet connection. We paid for every tool we tested out of pocket.

Each tool was scored on four dimensions:

  
- **Transcription accuracy (40%)** - Word Error Rate on a 1,200-word ground-truth sample we transcribed manually. Heavy accents, crosstalk, and poor mic quality are excluded from the headline number but reported separately.
  
- **Summary quality (25%)** - 1-5 manual rating by two reviewers on the same 10-meeting sample. We scored action-item extraction, decision capture, and topic segmentation.
  
- **Integration depth (20%)** - number of native connectors to Zoom / Meet / Teams / Webex, plus CRM sync to HubSpot / Salesforce / Pipedrive, plus push-to-Slack and Notion.
  
- **Pricing transparency (15%)** - does the published price reflect what you actually pay? Does the free tier work, or is it a crippled preview?

Two things to know going in: (1) every tool on this list joins your meeting as a notetaker bot by default - always disclose to attendees and check local consent laws; (2) the gap between the top 4 tools is small enough that integration and pricing matter more than raw accuracy once you clear the 90% accuracy bar.
