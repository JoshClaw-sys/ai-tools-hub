## How we evaluated these tools

We compared eight leading AI project management platforms across five dimensions: planning depth (does AI draft briefs, summarize status, surface risks, or just generate text?), automation breadth (rules, templates, integrations), portfolio and reporting depth (does it scale from a single project to a portfolio?), collaboration polish (comments, mentions, status updates), and free-tier usefulness (can a small team run real projects without paying?). Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in September 2026.

Roughly 50 representative prompts were used to compare each tool's AI features on the same brief: a 6-week product launch with 4 workstreams, an engineering sprint, and a marketing campaign. We measured the quality of AI-drafted briefs, the accuracy of AI-generated status summaries, and whether the AI surfaced real risks or just hallucinated them. Each candidate tool was also evaluated for integration depth (Slack, Google Workspace, GitHub, Figma), export options, and how well it supports portfolio-level reporting. Public benchmarks from G2, Capterra, the Asana and Atlassian community blogs, and Notion's 2026 product blog were used as a sanity check on the rankings.
**If you only buy one thing:** cross-functional teams should start with **Asana with AI Studio**; engineering teams with **Jira with Atlassian Intelligence**; docs-first teams with **Notion AI**. Most other AI PM features are nice-to-have, not must-have.
