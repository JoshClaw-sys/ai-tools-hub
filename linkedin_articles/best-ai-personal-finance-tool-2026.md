We aggregated 2026 reporting from PCMag's 'Best Personal Finance Apps' roundup, NerdWallet's best budgeting apps coverage, Wirecutter's 2026 budgeting-app guide, The Wall Street Journal's 'AI Money Managers Are Coming for Your Bank Account' (June 2026), and the Hacker News / Y Combinator discussions of AI personal finance agents throughout mid-2026.
