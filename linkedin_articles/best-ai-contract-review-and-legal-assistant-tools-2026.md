## How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 editorial reviews from LawSites, ABA Journal, Artificial Lawyer, and the 2026 Legalweek vendor evaluations. We signed up for free trials and demos with every tool. Pricing was verified against vendor-published rate cards in September 2026. Tools were evaluated across five dimensions: contract-review accuracy on real M&A and commercial-contract samples, Word/DocX integration depth, hallucination resistance (does it cite real case law vs invented), pricing transparency, and SOC 2 / data-handling compliance for client confidential data. Tools that failed basic hallucination-resistance testing on redlined contracts were downgraded regardless of feature breadth.
