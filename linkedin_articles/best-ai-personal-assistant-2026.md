## How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 reporting from Zapier's AI personal assistant roundup, The Verge's 2026 productivity-AI roundup, McKinsey's 2026 state-of-AI-work report, G2's Calendar Software category leaderboard (August 2026), and verified per-seat pricing from each vendor's public site. We cross-referenced every pricing model and AI feature cap against each vendor's live pricing page. This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark - pilot any AI personal assistant for at least 14 days against your own calendar before committing to annual contracts. Pricing verified September 2026.

The four axes we scored every tool on:

  
- **AI calendar accuracy** - does it actually respect deep-work blocks, lunch preferences, and meeting-free days when scheduling?
  
- **Free-tier usability** - is the free tier usable for an individual, or is it a crippled preview that forces an upgrade?
  
- **Integration breadth** - Google Calendar, Outlook, Slack, Zoom, Notion, Asana, Todoist
  
- **Team coordination features** - if you have a team, can you share calendars, see focus-time analytics, coordinate meetings across multiple users?
  
- **Mobile support** - does the iOS/Android app work offline, sync reliably, and feel native?
