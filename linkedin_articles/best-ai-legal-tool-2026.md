## How we picked these tools

We aggregated 2026 reviews from LegalTech News, Lawsite, ABA Journal, Vaquill, and CL Lawyers, cross-referenced each vendor's official pricing page, and compared the strengths and limits of each platform. Pricing was verified against vendor sites this month. Note that legal AI pricing in 2026 spans three orders of magnitude -- from $20/user/mo for individual contract review tools to $1,000+/user/mo for enterprise legal research platforms. This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark -- every vendor offers a demo or trial so you can verify fit for your practice area.

Tools were scored on five dimensions:

  
- **Legal AI accuracy** -- research citation accuracy, drafting quality, hallucination rate on case law.
  
- **Practice area fit** -- corporate, litigation, IP, employment, real estate -- does it cover the work you do?
  
- **Integration depth** -- with Westlaw, Lexis, iManage, NetDocuments, Microsoft 365.
  
- **Security and compliance** -- SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, attorney-client privilege protections.
  
- **Pricing value** -- cost per user including AI features, not just the sticker price.
