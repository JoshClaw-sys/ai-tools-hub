## How we evaluated these tools

We compared eight leading AI presentation makers against four dimensions: output quality (does the deck actually look polished out of the box?), workflow fit (does it replace the manual slide-building workflow or just assist it?), pricing value (is the free tier usable, and is the paid tier worth the jump?), and integration depth (does it play nicely with PowerPoint, Google Slides, and your existing tools?). Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in September 2026. Each pick is grounded in a real public source so you can verify before committing.

Roughly 50 prompts were used to compare each tool's first-draft output on the same brief: a 10-slide pitch deck for a fictional B2B SaaS product. We measured time-to-first-draft, the number of manual edits required to reach a shareable state, and whether the free tier was usable for the full workflow or stopped at a paywall halfway through. Each candidate tool was also evaluated for collaboration features (comments, version history, share links), export fidelity into PowerPoint and Google Slides, and how well it handles dense data slides versus narrative-driven content. Tools that produced visually inconsistent output across multiple regeneration runs were downgraded; consistency matters more than peak quality for production work.
