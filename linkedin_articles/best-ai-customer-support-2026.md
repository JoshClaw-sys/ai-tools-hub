## How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 reporting from Toolradar's AI-support ranking (August 2026), G2's summer 2026 leader quadrant for AI customer service, Crozdesk's help-desk AI category review, the E-commerce Tech AI support roundup, and Zendesk's CX-trends 2026 benchmark, then cross-referenced every plan tier, per-resolution pricing, free-tier agent quota, channel coverage, and deflection rate against each vendor's live pricing page. Where reviewers disagreed on resolution accuracy, hand-off quality, or onboarding speed we note both the consensus and the outlier.

This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark - every tool listed has a free tier or trial so you can validate fit on your own ticket queue. Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in August 2026.

Tools were scored on:

  
- **Resolution accuracy** - the share of tickets the AI agent resolves without a human.
  
- **Channel coverage** - how many messaging surfaces the AI agent handles (web, email, WhatsApp, SMS, voice).
  
- **Setup speed** - how quickly a working bot ships on a real knowledge base.
  
- **Pricing transparency** - whether the per-resolution or per-seat model surprises customers at scale.
  
- **Hand-off quality** - whether escalations to human agents retain context.
