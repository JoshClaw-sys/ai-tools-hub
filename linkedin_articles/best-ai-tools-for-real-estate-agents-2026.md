## 2. Follow Up Boss

Starter $49/mo / Professional $69/mo / Enterprise $99/mo

**Best for:** Teams and brokerages that need a single source of truth for lead management.

**Specs:**

- Lead routing with round-robin, source weighting, action plans

- AI Action Plans: automated 30/60/90-day text + email cadences

- 200+ lead source integrations (Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook, Google)

- Mobile dialer, call recording, transcription

- Reporting on agent activity, lead source ROI, conversion velocity

**Pros:**

- Best-in-class lead routing -- the rule engine is the deepest in the category

- AI Action Plans cut the manual drip campaign setup time by ~80%

- Native integrations with the major real estate lead sources

**Cons:**

- Starter tier caps lead volume; most teams
