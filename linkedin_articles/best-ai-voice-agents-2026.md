## How we picked (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 reviews from tested.media, brilo.ai, G2, and direct vendor documentation, then cross-referenced each platform's public pricing page and changelog. We compared the four leading platforms on six dimensions that actually matter to someone deploying an AI voice agent: voice naturalness, latency at the 95th percentile, tool-call reliability, integration depth, time to first call, and total cost of ownership across three volume tiers.

Voice naturalness scores come from independent 2026 listening tests where reviewers rated "definitely AI" vs "could be human" on sample agent responses. Latency and tool-call accuracy numbers come from published vendor benchmarks and third-party platform comparisons. Pricing was verified against each vendor's public pricing page on September 21,2026.

This is not a hands-on benchmark — we did not place 10,000 test calls through each platform. The right next step for any of these picks is to claim the free trial credit (most offer $10 to start) and validate the platform against your actual call flows before committing. Vendor-published benchmarks tend to overstate real-world performance, especially under load.
