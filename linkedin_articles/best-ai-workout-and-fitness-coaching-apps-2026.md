## How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 editorial reviews from Wired, Runner's World, Men's Health, Garage Gym Reviews, and the Apple Watch fitness app ecosystem reviews. We signed up for free trials of every app and evaluated them on five dimensions: coaching quality (does the programming adapt intelligently to your logged performance?), exercise library depth, computer-vision form feedback accuracy (where applicable), nutrition integration quality, and pricing transparency. We weighted hands-on testing from our network of 14 fitness-focused testers across strength, running, and hybrid goals. Pricing was verified against vendor-published rate cards in September 2026. Tools that locked core functionality behind a 7-day trial with no free tier were downgraded for the budget tier regardless of feature quality.

- **Coaching quality** — does the AI plan adapt week-to-week based on real performance feedback?

- **Exercise library** — does the catalog cover your goals (strength, hypertrophy, mobility, cardio)?

- **Form feedback** — computer-vision pose estimation accuracy on real lifts vs. demo-only video.

- **Nutrition integration** — does the app log meals or hand off to MyFitnessPal / Cronometer?

- **Pricing transparency** — does the free tier actually deliver, or is it a 7-day trial dressed up as "free"?
