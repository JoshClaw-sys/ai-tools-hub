## The Picks

## Suno v5 — Best for full songs with vocals

**Pricing:** Free / Pro $10/mo / Premier $30/mo

**Best for:** Creators who want full song structure (verse, chorus, bridge) with realistic vocal generation.

**Why it wins:** Suno v5 produces the most listenable AI music in 2026. Vocal phrasing, emotional delivery, and breath placement all hold up. Song structure is real — verses build into choruses with melodic variation. Stem export on the Premier tier lets you pull vocals, drums, bass, and instruments separately for remixing in a DAW.

The 2026 release added Suno Studio, a multi-track editor that lets you refine generations with arrangement controls. Suno's distribution to Spotify and Apple Music through the Premier tier is the cleanest in the category.

**Trade-offs:** Ongoing UMG and Sony lawsuits create real commercial-use risk. Suno's training data history is disputed, and licensing terms have shifted multiple times in 2025–2026. Watermarks appear on free-tier outputs.

## Udio v1.5 — Best for high-quality vocals and remixing

**Pricing:** Free / Starter $10/mo / Professional $30/mo

**Best for:** Creators who want long-form tracks and strong remix features.

**Why it wins:** Udio v1.5 matches Suno v5 on vocal realism and supports tracks up to 15 minutes — twice Suno's length cap. The remix features let you take an existing track and extend, restyle, or rework it. Stem separation works cleanly.

**Trade-offs:** Udio is also subject to UMG and Sony lawsuits. The UI is less polished than Suno, and the learning curve is steeper.
