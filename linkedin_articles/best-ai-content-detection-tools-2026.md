## How we picked these detectors

This is a research roundup, not a hands-on benchmark. We aggregated 2026 reviews and pricing from CyberNested, EyeSift, and the official pricing pages for each detector (rechecked June 2026). We focused on four signals: detection accuracy on unedited AI samples, accuracy drop on edited content, free-tier generosity, and feature depth (plagiarism, OCR, LMS integrations, audit logs).

The detectors were scored on:

  
- **Accuracy on unedited AI content** - how well the detector catches fresh GPT-4o output
  
- **Accuracy on human-written content** - false-positive rate
  
- **Free tier** - what you can do without paying
  
- **Feature depth** - plagiarism, OCR, batch upload, LMS, audit logs
  
- **Documentation clarity** - credit math, plan structure, API contracts

Pricing was verified against each vendor's official pricing page on June 11, 2026. Verify live pricing before committing - detectors restructure plans frequently.
