## How we evaluated these tools

We compared eight leading AI sales tools and CRMs across four dimensions: AI lead-scoring accuracy (does it surface real intent signals, or just demographic fit?), conversation intelligence quality (transcription, summaries, action items), pipeline automation depth (workflows, sequences, deal-rot alerts), and integration coverage (Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Zoom, LinkedIn). Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in September 2026. We also weighed free-tier usefulness - whether a solo rep or a 3-person team can run a real pipeline without paying anything.

Each tool was compared against roughly 40 representative prompts and tasks: lead-scoring calibration on a sample inbound list, post-call summary quality across a recorded Zoom call, draft-email quality on a follow-up sequence, and pipeline hygiene on a 90-day deal set. Tools were downgraded if their AI features required expensive add-ons, if free tiers locked core CRM functionality behind a paywall, or if integration depth was thin. Public benchmarks from G2, Capterra, Zapier, and the Salesforce 2026 product blog were used as a sanity check on the rankings.
