## How we picked these tools

We aggregated 2026 reviews from G2, Capterra, PCMag, and ToolixLab, cross-referenced each vendor's official pricing page, and compared the strengths and limits of each platform. Pricing was verified against vendor sites this month. Note that in 2026 most PM vendors shifted to per-AI-credit pricing on top of seat fees -- ClickUp Brain credits, Asana AI Studio usage caps, and Monday AI Blocks each meter AI usage separately. This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark -- every vendor listed offers a free trial so you can verify fit before committing.

Tools were scored on five dimensions:

  
- **AI depth** -- summarization, content generation, action-taking AI, and natural-language automations.
  
- **Workflow fit** -- how well the underlying PM platform supports real team workflows (tasks, docs, dashboards, dependencies).
  
- **Pricing value** -- cost per user including AI features, not just the seat price.
  
- **Integration depth** -- native integrations with Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Figma, and the rest of a typical SaaS stack.
  
- **Ease of use** -- how quickly a non-expert team can get value in the first week.
