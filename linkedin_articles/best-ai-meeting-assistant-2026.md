## How we picked these tools

We aggregated 2026 reviews from zapier.com, brixi.ai, and meetingnotes.com, cross-referenced each vendor's official pricing page, and compared transcription accuracy (where published), pricing tiers, and integration depth. This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark — every tool listed has a usable free tier so you can verify fit before committing.

What we looked for:

  
- **Real 2026 pricing** — verified against each vendor's official pricing page this month, including free tier limits and team pricing.
  
- **Transcription accuracy** — published word-error-rate benchmarks where available, with a note on English vs. multilingual performance.
  
- **Free tier quality** — minute caps, recording limits, and whether the free tier is a usable product or just a preview.
  
- **CRM and collaboration integrations** — Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion — does it drop notes where your team works?
  
- **Bot presence** — some tools join your meeting as a visible bot, others stay silent. The silent mode is increasingly important in 2026.
