## How we tested (methodology)

We did not run a hands-on benchmark. Instead, we aggregated 2026 reviews from ChatCut, Eklipse, aitoolradar.net, Dupple, get-alfred.ai, and Reclaim's Motion alternatives post, then cross-referenced each tool's pricing, licensing terms, feature limits, and integration list against the vendor's own pricing page in August 2026. Where multiple reviewers disagreed (for example on whether CapCut's AI features are pro-grade or just polished), we noted the consensus and the outlier. For every tool, we asked the same four questions:

  
- **Does it actually do the job?** -- does the headline AI feature deliver in real workflows, or only in cherry-picked demos?
  
- **Is the AI worth paying for over the free tier?** -- many tools now bundle AI into the base plan, which changes the math.
  
- **Does it integrate with what you already use?** -- a video editor that cannot export to Premiere is a dead end for agencies.
  
- **Is the pricing honest?** -- "from $X" with a credit system that doubles your bill at typical usage is a dealbreaker we flag explicitly.
