## How we picked these frameworks

This is a research roundup, not a hands-on benchmark. We aggregated 2026 reviews and comparisons from Trendix, aiagentslist.com, and the official project documentation for each framework. We focused on three signals: production-readiness (does the framework ship with observability, durability, and rollback), ecosystem health (GitHub stars, recent releases, enterprise adoption), and architectural fit (which framework matches which kind of workflow).

The frameworks were scored on:

  
- **Production-readiness** - enterprise deployments, observability, durable execution
  
- **Ecosystem health** - release cadence, community size, vendor support
  
- **Architectural clarity** - does the mental model map to your workflow
  
- **Ease of onboarding** - time to first useful agent
  
- **Vendor lock-in risk** - can you migrate off without rewriting everything

Pricing was verified against each vendor's official website as of August 2026. Verify live pricing before committing to a paid tier - plan structures and seat pricing change frequently.
