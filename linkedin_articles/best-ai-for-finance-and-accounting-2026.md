## How we evaluated these tools

We compared twelve leading AI tools across four dimensions: accuracy (does the AI categorization match what a senior bookkeeper would do?), integration depth (does it plug cleanly into QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, and Sage without breaking the audit trail?), autonomy (assisted vs fully autonomous approval flows), and audit/compliance posture (SOC 2, audit logs, data residency). Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in September 2026. Each pick is grounded in a public source so you can verify before committing.

We deliberately avoided any first-hand testing claims. AI accounting tools should be evaluated by a qualified CPA against your specific workflow, not by an editor guessing at edge cases. Our evaluation also surfaced some practical considerations that often get lost in marketing copy: how each tool handles period-close cutoffs, whether categorization suggestions can be batch-rejected, how vendor-master duplicates are surfaced, and whether historical transactions can be re-classified in bulk when the AI's confidence improves. Tools that lacked clean re-classification flows were downgraded, because real bookkeeping work means updating old entries as the AI learns your chart of accounts over time.
