## Quick verdict — our top pick

AI sales coaching tools in 2026 fall into three buckets: full conversation-intelligence platforms (Gong, Chorus), lighter call-recording + AI-notes tools (Avoma, tl;dv), and CRM-native coaching modules (HubSpot, Salesforce Einstein). Picking the right one depends on team size, CRM stack, and whether you need manager-facing dashboards or rep-facing scorecards.

**Gong** remains the strongest all-around AI sales coaching platform in 2026 — deal intelligence, conversation analytics, and manager coaching workflows in one stack. For lean teams that only need call recording and basic scorecards, **Salesloft Drift** and **Avoma** cover 80% of the value at roughly half the per-seat price.

**Best for most people:** the top pick below.
**Best for budget:** one of the free or low-cost options in the comparison.
**Best for teams / enterprise:** a tier with admin controls, SSO, and audit logs.

**Key takeaways:**

- **Gong** leads on conversation intelligence and deal-risk analytics; the cost is justified above ~10 reps.

- **Salesloft** remains the strongest workflow + dialer combo; Drift (Salesloft acquired) is the conversational piece.

- **Avoma** is the best mid-market value — call recording, AI notes, and coaching scorecards under $30/user/mo.

- **Chorus (ZoomInfo)**, **Outreach**, and **HubSpot Sales Hub** round out the top tier with deeper CRM-native workflows.
