## How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 reporting from Flowith's Gauth alternatives guide (March 2026), Cybernews's AI homework helper ranking (April 2026), EduAI's homework helper category review, Creative Tools AI's directory of AI homework tools, and SpellingJoy's homework-helper buyer guide, then cross-referenced every plan tier, per-month price, free-tier quota, subject coverage, and step-by-step solution quality against each vendor's live site. Where reviewers disagreed on solution quality, language support, or tutor handoff we note both the consensus and the outlier.

This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark - every tool listed has a free tier so you can validate fit on your own homework. Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in August 2026.

Tools were scored on:

  
- **Step-by-step solution quality** - whether the explanation walks through the steps or just returns the answer.
  
- **Subject coverage** - math only, math + science, or all subjects.
  
- **Free-tier usability** - whether the free tier is enough for everyday homework.
  
- **Input flexibility** - photo input, typed input, or both.
  
- **Tutor handoff** - whether the AI hands off to a human tutor when stuck.
