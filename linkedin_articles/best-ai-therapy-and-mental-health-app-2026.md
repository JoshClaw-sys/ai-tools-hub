## Quick verdict — our top pick

AI mental health apps in 2026 fall into four categories: clinically-validated CBT chatbots (Wysa, Woebot), mood-tracking companions (Earkick, Mindstrong), general AI companions that include mental health features (Replika), and therapist-augmentation tools (Lyssn). Picking the right one depends on what kind of support you want and whether you need human escalation paths.

**Wysa** remains the most clinically-validated AI mental health app in 2026 — CBT-based chatbot, mood tracking, and human-coach escalation paths in one app. For users who want a warmer AI-only experience without the clinical framing, **Earkick** and **Woebot** are the strongest standalone picks.

**Best for most people:** the top pick below.
**Best for budget:** one of the free or low-cost options in the comparison.
**Best for teams / enterprise:** a tier with admin controls, SSO, and audit logs.

**Key takeaways:**

- **Wysa** has the strongest clinical evidence base (multiple RCTs) and human-coach escalation.

- **Woebot** is the simplest, fastest CBT chat experience — best for users who want low-friction daily check-ins.

- **Earkick** is the best consumer-grade AI mood tracker with multi-modal input (voice, photo, text).

- **Youper**, **Replika**, and **Mindstrong** round out the category with deeper personalization.
