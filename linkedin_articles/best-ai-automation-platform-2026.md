## How we picked these platforms

We aggregated 2026 reviews from technologyadvice.com, neuralcoretech.com, and taskade.com, cross-referenced each platform's official pricing page, and compared the strengths and limits of each by team technical profile (no-code SMB / developer-led / enterprise iPaaS). Where reviewers disagreed, we noted the consensus and the outlier. This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark — every platform listed has a free tier or trial so you can verify fit before committing.

What we looked for:

  
- **Real 2026 pricing** — verified against each vendor's official pricing page this month, not a stale affiliate article.
  
- **Team technical profile fit** — non-technical team → start with Zapier. Operations team with some technical comfort → Make. Developer-led team or AI pipeline ambitions → n8n. Enterprise with legacy desktop systems → UiPath.
  
- **AI depth** — does the platform have native LLM nodes, or are AI features bolted on as a paid add-on?
  
- **Data residency** — for GDPR, HIPAA, or EU data residency, only n8n self-hosted and UiPath on-premise provide infrastructure-level control.
  
- **Honest limits** — every platform has them; we call out the data residency cliff, the pricing cliff, and the connector gap.
