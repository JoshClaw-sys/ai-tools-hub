## How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 reporting from Toolradar's AI tutor ranking (August 2026), the Khan Academy Khanmigo 2026 impact report, Airtable's 8-tutor editor review, the AI Rankings June 2026 study-tools comparison, and Notesmakr's AI study-app guide, then cross-referenced every plan tier, per-month price, free-tier quota, subject coverage, and Socratic vs answer-mode behavior against each vendor's live site. Where reviewers disagreed on subject coverage, language support, or onboarding we note both the consensus and the outlier.

This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark - every tool listed has a free tier or trial so you can validate fit on your own subject area. Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in August 2026.

Tools were scored on:

  
- **Pedagogy** - whether the tutor guides (Socratic) or hands over answers (answer-machine).
  
- **Subject coverage** - how wide the subject range is (math, science, humanities, writing, coding).
  
- **Pricing fairness** - whether the free tier is usable, and whether the paid tier is reasonable.
  
- **Setup speed** - how quickly a working tutor is live on a real subject.
  
- **Grounded accuracy** - whether answers cite sources or are pulled from a closed corpus.
