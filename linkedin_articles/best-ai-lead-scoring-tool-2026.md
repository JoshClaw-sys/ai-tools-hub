We aggregated 2026 reporting from G2's Lead Scoring category leaderboard (August 2026), HubSpot's AI lead scoring buyer's guide, Salesforce Einstein Lead Scoring documentation, MadKudu's 2026 B2B lead scoring playbook, 6sense's 2026 intent-data report, and Gartner's 2026 Magic Quadrant for ABM platforms. The four axes we scored every tool on:

  
- **AI scoring accuracy** - does the model train on your own closed deals, or generic industry averages? Can admins inspect feature weights?
  
- **Data freshness** - how often does the score refresh? Weekly, daily, hourly, real-time?
  
- **Intent-data depth** - does the platform bundle first-party intent signals, or require a separate Bombora / G2 Buyer Intent subscription?
  
- **Sales handoff** - does the scored lead auto-route to the right rep or workflow, or does the team need to manually check the score?
  
- **Per-seat pricing fairness** - is the floor reasonable for the value delivered, or enterprise-only?
