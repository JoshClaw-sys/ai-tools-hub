## How we tested (methodology)

We tested each tool against the same five real-world editing tasks:

  
- **Remove a person from a wedding photo** — using Generative Remove / Inpaint tools. We graded on whether the removed region blended naturally with the surrounding scene.
  
- **Replace a flat sky with a sunset** — using Generative Replace / Sky Replace. We graded on lighting consistency and edge quality.
  
- **Expand a portrait from square to 16:9** — using Generative Expand / Outpaint. We graded on identity preservation of the subject and how natural the extended scene looked.
  
- **Swap a product background for e-commerce** — using Background Replace. We graded on edge quality around hair/cloth and color matching.
  
- **Apply a consistent style across a 10-image product set** — using Style Reference / Brand Kit. We graded on consistency and whether the style survived across all 10 images.

We graded on:

  
- **Output quality** — does the result look professional, or does it have obvious AI artifacts?
  
- **Identity preservation** — does the human subject still look like themselves?
  
- **Manual control** — can I refine the AI output with traditional tools (masks, brushes, layers)?
  
- **Batch support** — can I apply the same edit to 10+ images without manually repeating each step?
  
- **Export** — what formats can I export, and at what quality?
