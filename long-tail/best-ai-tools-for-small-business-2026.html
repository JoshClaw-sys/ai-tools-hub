❓ Frequently asked questions

How much should a small business spend on AI tools?

$50-150/mo per team member is the sweet spot. Below $50/mo you're missing critical tools. Above $200/mo you're paying for features you don't use.

Which single AI tool should I start with?

ChatGPT Team. It's the most versatile — covers writing, analysis, code review, and customer support drafts. $25/user/month, cancel anytime.

Do I need AI customer support?

If you get more than 50 tickets/day, yes. Tidio or Intercom Fin handles the routine 80% automatically and saves you from hiring a support rep.
