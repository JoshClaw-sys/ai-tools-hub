Pricing and plan notes that matter

Metered vs flat pricing. Most AI app and chatbot builders in 2026 charge by usage (credits, tokens, messages, or seats), not by a flat monthly fee. The headline price is the floor - heavy iteration or high message volume pushes the bill higher. Adalo ($36/mo flat) and Mocha are the rare exceptions for predictable budgeting. When evaluating any builder, read the pricing-page footnote, not the headline.

The white-label trap for agencies. If you're an agency deploying chatbots or apps for multiple clients, white-label branding add-on costs matter. Chatbase charges $199/mo for white-label. SiteGPT charges $39/mo for the same feature. Botpress offers full white-label through self-hosting but requires engineering work. This single line item can be the difference between a profitable agency and a money-losing one.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

The free tiers on this list are actually usable for validation in 2026. Botpress free covers 500 messages/mo, Chatbase free covers 100 messages/mo + 1 chatbot, ManyChat free covers 1,000 contacts, and Tidio free covers basic live chat. The free tiers are tight enough that high-engagement sites hit limits fast - expect to upgrade within 60 days if you see real value from the bot.

Three thresholds that justify a paid tier: (a) message volume above 500/mo (Botpress Plus at $89/mo is the cheapest entry that handles production traffic), (b) white-label or custom branding need (SiteGPT's $39/mo add-on is the cheapest path), (c) team collaboration with admin controls (Standard tier or above on any platform).

Frequently asked questions

Which AI chatbot builder is best for an enterprise support team?

Botpress is the best enterprise pick in 2026 - the Plus tier at $89/mo (5,000 messages) is the most transparent enterprise-grade option, the workflow + integration coverage is the deepest in the category, and it's the only builder on this list with real open-source extensibility for custom integrations. SiteGPT is the alternative if your priority is white-label chatbots for multiple clients - its $39/mo white-label add-on is significantly cheaper than Chatbase's $199/mo for the same feature.

How much does an AI chatbot builder really cost?

For most small businesses, expect $40-$89/mo for the entry tier that covers production traffic. SiteGPT's $39/mo Starter and Chatbase's $40/mo Hobby are the cheapest production-grade entries. Botpress Plus at $89/mo is the best enterprise value - 5,000 messages with most features unlocked. For very high volume (10,000+ messages/mo), expect $200-$500/mo on any platform, and add LLM API costs on top for Botpress (which bills OpenAI and Anthropic usage separately). Most builders offer a free tier that covers 100-1,000 messages/mo for testing before you commit.

Do these chatbot builders work for Instagram and WhatsApp?

ManyChat is the best Instagram DM and WhatsApp builder on this list - the flow builder is purpose-built for social channels and the free tier covers 1,000 contacts. Tidio supports Instagram + Messenger + email but not native WhatsApp. Chatbase and Botpress are primarily web-chat tools - Botpress can integrate with WhatsApp Business API but requires custom setup. SiteGPT focuses on web chat with content ingestion. For a marketing funnel bot on social channels, ManyChat wins; for support bots on your website, the other four are stronger.

Can a small business start with a free AI chatbot builder?

Yes - Botpress free (500 messages/mo), Chatbase free (100 messages/mo + 1 chatbot), ManyChat free (1,000 contacts), and Tidio free (basic live chat with limited AI) all cover small businesses getting started. The free tiers are genuinely usable for under-50-conversation-per-day stores and for validating whether an AI chatbot helps your conversion before you commit to a paid plan. The honest answer is that most businesses that see real value from a chatbot upgrade to a paid tier within 60 days - the free tiers are tight enough that high-engagement sites hit limits fast.

Which chatbot builder has the best white-label option?

SiteGPT has the best white-label option in 2026 - $39/mo add-on removes all SiteGPT branding and lets agencies deploy fully branded chatbots for clients. Chatbase's white-label is $199/mo (5x more expensive) and is tied to the Standard tier and above. Botpress's open-source SDK allows full white-label through self-hosting but requires engineering work. Tidio and ManyChat do not offer white-label at any tier. For an agency deploying chatbots for multiple clients, SiteGPT is the clearest answer.

Which chatbot builder is best for a Shopify store?

Tidio is the best Shopify-specific choice - native Shopify integration ingests products, collections, and order data; the Lyro AI ships a working bot in under an hour; and $29/mo entry pricing is reasonable for small stores. ManyChat is the right alternative if your Shopify store does most of its customer engagement on Instagram DM. Chatbase is a strong FAQ-bot option if your Shopify questions are mostly catalog/policy related - train it on your store's help docs and it handles the rest.
