Who should (and shouldn't) buy

AI notetaking apps hit a sweet spot for three buyer profiles and a poor fit for two. The strong fits are: (a) anyone sitting in 5+ meetings per week who needs searchable transcripts and AI summaries, (b) sales and customer-success teams that need meeting archives synced to a CRM, and (c) Notion-first or ClickUp-first teams who want AI inside the existing docs and project-management workflow.

The weak fits are: (a) anyone who sits in 0-2 meetings per week - Otter.ai's free tier or Mem.ai's free tier covers manual note-taking at zero cost, and (b) Apple Notes loyalists who refuse to adopt a second app - Mem.ai's self-organizing model is the closest fit at $14.99/mo.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

The category has three pricing models: free tier with usage caps (Otter.ai 300 min/mo), per-user add-on to an existing tool (Notion AI $10/mo, ClickUp AI $7/mo per user), and standalone subscription (Fireflies.ai $18/mo, Sunsama $20/mo, Mem.ai $14.99/mo). The cheapest path is to add AI onto a tool you already pay for - Notion AI and ClickUp AI are the lowest-cost options for teams already on those platforms.

Watch for the usage caps on free tiers. Otter.ai's free 300 min/mo is generous but caps each meeting at 30 minutes. Fireflies.ai's free tier has the tightest transcription caps in the category. Sunsama and Mem.ai ship no permanent free tier (Sunsama ships a 14-day trial, Mem.ai ships a limited free tier).

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

If you sit in 5+ meetings per week, Otter.ai's free tier covers the floor for testing; upgrade to Pro at $16.99/mo when you need meetings longer than 30 minutes or AI summaries. If your team runs on Notion or ClickUp, the $7-$10/mo AI add-on is the cheapest path. If you want CRM sync, Fireflies.ai at $18/mo is the only option. Skip Mem.ai unless you want self-organizing notes without tagging or folders.
