Who should buy what

Categorical recommendations based on the workflow stage:

  • European business translation — DeepL wins on WMT24 benchmarks for EN/DE/FR/ES/IT/PT. The Pro tier at ~$8.74/mo is the best value.
  • Asian language translation — ChatGPT produces the most natural output for JA/ZH/KO. Claude is the runner-up for long documents.
  • Free, broad coverage — Google Translate is unbeatable for casual translation across 133+ languages. No account required.
  • Creative and marketing copy — ChatGPT or Claude. Both let you prompt for tone, style, and cultural adaptation in ways that specialized engines cannot.
  • Team-scale localization — Smartcat is the only tool that manages translation memory, vendor coordination, and CMS integration in one platform.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

Translation tool pricing in 2026 is unusually fragmented — some tools are free for individual use but charge for API, others are free per request but cap at a monthly character count, and LLM-backed tools are billed per token. A few things to watch for:

  • DeepL free tier — 500K characters per month on the free tier is generous for casual use. The Pro tier (~$8.74/mo) removes the cap and adds glossary control.
  • Google Translate — free for personal use with no meaningful limits on the web and app. The Cloud Translation API is $20 per 1M characters for paid use.
  • ChatGPT API — billed per token, not per character. Translation costs roughly $30-$60 per 1M characters depending on the model. Less predictable than DeepL's flat fee.
  • Claude API — $3/$15 per 1M input/output tokens for Sonnet 4.5. Comparable to ChatGPT for most use cases, but with a larger context window for long documents.
  • Smartcat — has a free tier but paid team plans are custom-quoted. Worth the conversation only if you are doing ongoing translation at team scale.

Quality vs cost: the real tradeoff

DeepL often beats apparently cheaper options in real business use because fewer errors mean less re-editing. Per-character API costs look similar — about $5-$25 per 1M characters for DeepL, $20 for Google, $30-$60 for LLMs — but the per-translation cost including human review is significantly lower on DeepL for European business content. For casual, low-stakes translation, Google Translate's free web tier is unbeatable. Model your monthly volume and quality requirements rather than choosing on headline price alone.
