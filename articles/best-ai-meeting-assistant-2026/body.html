Quick verdict — our top pick

AI meeting assistants in 2026 have settled into four camps: free-and-generous (Fathom), sales-native (Fireflies), accuracy leaders (Otter), and clip-sharing pros (tl;dv). The right pick depends on what kind of meetings you sit in and whether you need team-wide deployment. This roundup covers the seven platforms that showed up consistently in 2026 buyer guides.

TL;DR: Fathom — best AI meeting assistant in 2026 for most people. Unlimited free recording and transcription for Zoom/Meet/Teams, no time caps, no credit card. If you run a sales team that needs CRM logging, switch to Fireflies at $10/user/mo. If you need multilingual accuracy above 95%, switch to Otter.

Best for most people: Fathom (free).
Best for sales teams: Fireflies.ai ($10/user/mo).
Best for top accuracy: Otter Pro ($20/user/mo).
Best for clip sharing: tl;dv ($20/user/mo).

How we picked these tools

We aggregated 2026 reviews from zapier.com, brixi.ai, and meetingnotes.com, cross-referenced each vendor's official pricing page, and compared transcription accuracy (where published), pricing tiers, and integration depth. This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark — every tool listed has a usable free tier so you can verify fit before committing.

What we looked for:

  • Real 2026 pricing — verified against each vendor's official pricing page this month, including free tier limits and team pricing.
  • Transcription accuracy — published word-error-rate benchmarks where available, with a note on English vs. multilingual performance.
  • Free tier quality — minute caps, recording limits, and whether the free tier is a usable product or just a preview.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI meeting assistant in 2026?

For most people, Fathom is the best AI meeting assistant in 2026 — unlimited free recording and transcription across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams with no time caps. For sales teams that need CRM logging, Fireflies at $10/user/mo is the better pick. For top-tier English accuracy and multilingual support, Otter Pro is the leader.

Is there a free AI meeting assistant?

Yes — Fathom's free tier is the most generous in the category: unlimited recording, transcription, and AI summaries with no minute caps and no credit card required. Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv, and Read.ai also offer free tiers but with stricter limits (300-1,000 minutes/mo typically).

How accurate is AI meeting transcription in 2026?

Independent 2026 benchmarks put Otter Pro at the top with ~96% word accuracy on English, Fireflies at ~94%, and Fathom at ~90-92%. Accuracy drops 5-15 percentage points on multilingual content and on poor audio. All major tools handle Zoom/Meet/Teams well; phone calls and in-person recording are weaker across the board.

What is the cheapest AI meeting assistant?

Fathom is free for unlimited use, which makes it the cheapest by default. Among paid tiers, Fireflies starts at $10/user/mo and Notta starts at $8.99/mo for basic transcription. Below $8/user/mo, quality and integration depth drop sharply.

Do AI meeting assistants work with Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?

Yes — Fathom, Otter, Fireflies, and tl;dv all work with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams in 2026. Some tools (Notta, Krisp) also support Webex and dial-in calls. In-person recording requires the vendor's mobile app or a hardware solution like a limit-less pendant.
