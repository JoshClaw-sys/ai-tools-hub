Who should buy what

Pick DeepL if: your translation work is mostly European languages and you want the highest-quality output for the price. DeepL Pro at $9/mo is the right entry tier for freelancers.

Pick Google Cloud Translation if: you need global coverage across 100+ languages, especially Asian or African pairs, and you have developer resources to wire the API.

Pick Smartling if: you run a translation agency or in-house localization team and need a TMS that combines AI output with human review + publishing workflows.

Pick ChatGPT Plus if: your translation is mostly ad-hoc -- short documents, emails, or research notes -- and you want the strongest context window.

Pick Microsoft Translator if: your translation volume is low and you're on the Microsoft stack -- the 2M char/mo free tier covers most personal use.

Skip Amazon Translate unless you're all-in on AWS -- Google Cloud Translation is cheaper, broader in language coverage, and has more mature custom model tooling.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI translation tool in 2026?

DeepL is the best for European languages (English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Dutch) -- it consistently wins blind quality tests against Google and ChatGPT on these pairs. For global coverage of 130+ languages including many Asian and African languages, Google Cloud Translation is the stronger choice.

Is AI translation accurate enough for business use?

For most business content (emails, marketing copy, internal docs, support tickets), AI translation is now at 90-95% accuracy for common language pairs and ready to ship after light editing. For legal, medical, and certified documents, AI output should always be reviewed by a human translator -- the cost of an error is too high.

How much do AI translation tools cost?

DeepL Free covers short docs (1,500 chars at a time) -- enough for testing. DeepL Pro is $9/mo for 100,000 chars/mo, scaling to enterprise tiers. Google Cloud Translation charges $20 per million characters. ChatGPT Plus at $20/mo has no per-character cap but slower throughput for large jobs.

Can AI translation handle technical jargon and brand voice?

Only with proper glossary setup. DeepL and Smartling both support custom glossaries that lock down product names, technical terms, and brand voice across all translations. Without a glossary, the AI will freely translate your brand name (a frequent failure). Set up glossaries before publishing anything in another language.

Do AI translation tools support 100+ languages?

Google Cloud Translation supports 130+ languages including low-resource ones (Swahili, Yoruba, Nepali, Khmer). DeepL covers 32 (mostly European + a few Asian). ChatGPT is strongest in the 50 most-common languages but degrades on long-tail pairs.

Which AI translator is best for ecommerce product listings?

DeepL Pro + Smartling is the strongest stack for ecommerce -- DeepL for the translation quality, Smartling for the workflow (glossary, reviewer approval, push to Shopify/WooCommerce). For pure cost-per-character at scale, Google Cloud Translation is cheaper ($20/M chars vs DeepL's $25/M).
