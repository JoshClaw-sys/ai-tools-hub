The picks

Six AI spreadsheet tools stood out in our 2026 research. Pricing ranges from Formula Bot's $8.33/mo annual to Microsoft Copilot for Microsoft 365's $30/user/mo. The picks below cover the five most common buyer profiles: enterprise on Microsoft 365, SMB on Google Workspace, analyst handling messy CSV uploads, finance team needing very long context, and non-technical user who wants dashboards and one-pagers.

Microsoft Copilot in Excel Best overall

Price: Free tier inside Microsoft 365 (limited prompts); Copilot Pro $20/user/mo with a Microsoft 365 Personal/Family subscription; Copilot for Microsoft 365 $30/user/mo (enterprise, requires 300-seat minimum) · Best for: Enterprise and SMB teams already on Microsoft 365 who want AI formulas, pivot summaries, and chart generation native to Excel

Microsoft Copilot in Excel is the deepest AI integration inside Excel in 2026 - it generates Python-powered formulas from natural-language prompts, summarizes entire workbooks, builds pivot tables, and creates charts without leaving the spreadsheet. The Copilot Pro tier at $20/user/mo unlocks unlimited prompts for individuals; Copilot for Microsoft 365 at $30/user/mo adds enterprise data-loss-prevention and Teams integration. Copilot reads column headers, named ranges, and Power Query connections, so prompts like 'summarize Q3 sales by region as a pivot' return usable output on the first try. For teams already paying for Microsoft 365, this is the lowest-friction path to AI in spreadsheets.

Key specs

  • Copilot Pro $20/user/mo
  • Copilot 365 $30/user/mo
  • Python formulas
  • Native Excel integration
  • Power Query aware

What works

  • Deepest native Excel integration - reads named ranges, tables, Power Query connections
  • Python-powered formulas handle complex logic that classic Excel formulas cannot
  • Copilot for Microsoft 365 at $30/user/mo adds enterprise data-loss-prevention
  • Summarizes entire workbooks with one prompt (best for finance/ops monthly close)

What to watch

  • Requires a Microsoft 365 subscription on top of the Copilot fee
  • Copilot for Microsoft 365 has a 300-seat minimum - blocks small teams
  • Free tier inside Microsoft 365 only ships a handful of prompts
  • Outputs occasionally need cleanup on large workbooks (10MB+ files)

Google Sheets + Gemini

Price: Free tier inside Google Workspace (limited Gemini prompts); Google AI Plus $9.99/user/mo (Workspace add-on) for higher Gemini limits; Google AI Ultra $249.99/user/mo for full Gemini 3 Pro access · Best for: Teams on Google Workspace who want AI-generated formulas, charts, and image generation directly inside Sheets

Google Sheets + Gemini is the strongest AI-spreadsheet pick for Google Workspace shops in 2026 - the Gemini side panel generates formulas, charts, summaries, and even images from natural-language prompts without leaving the sheet. The Workspace AI add-on at $9.99/user/mo (Google AI Plus) raises Gemini limits; Google AI Ultra at $249.99/user/mo unlocks the full Gemini 3 Pro model. Sheets + Gemini handles cross-sheet lookups, conditional formatting rules from prose ('highlight rows where revenue dropped >20% QoQ'), and inline images via Imagen 3. The pick for any team already paying for Google Workspace.

Key specs

  • AI Plus $9.99/user/mo
  • AI Ultra $249.99/user/mo
  • Gemini 3 Pro access
  • Imagen 3 inline images
  • Workspace-native

What works

  • Cheapest upgrade path at $9.99/user/mo for Google Workspace shops
  • Imagen 3 generates inline images from cell prompts ('add a chart icon here')
  • Native cross-sheet lookups via Google Sheets functions + Gemini
  • Conditional formatting from prose ('highlight rows where revenue dropped QoQ')

What to watch

  • Weaker than Copilot on complex Python-level spreadsheet logic
  • AI Ultra at $249.99/user/mo is enterprise-priced
  • Limited offline support vs Microsoft Excel
  • Formula accuracy lags Copilot on workbooks with >50 columns

ChatGPT (Plus / Team) with Advanced Data Analysis

Price: ChatGPT Plus $20/mo (GPT-5, Advanced Data Analysis, 3 file uploads per chat); ChatGPT Team $30/mo per user (GPT-5 Pro, higher limits, workspace sharing); ChatGPT Enterprise custom pricing · Best for: Analysts who need to upload messy CSVs and get clean, formatted Excel output back without learning Python

ChatGPT Plus with Advanced Data Analysis (formerly Code Interpreter) is the most versatile AI spreadsheet tool in 2026 - upload a CSV, ask 'split this into one sheet per region and add a totals row,' and the model writes Python, runs it, and returns a formatted XLSX. Plus at $20/mo unlocks GPT-5 with the data-analysis sandbox; Team at $30/mo per user adds GPT-5 Pro and workspace sharing. ChatGPT is the strongest pick when your source data lives outside Excel (a vendor CSV, a scraped HTML table, a PDF) and you want AI to do the cleaning, transformation, and charting end-to-end.

Claude (Pro / Team) with Artifacts

Price: Claude Pro $20/mo (5x usage, Artifacts, Projects workspaces); Claude Team $30/mo per user (more usage, admin tools); Claude Enterprise custom pricing (1M-token context) · Best for: Analysts who need very long context windows on multi-sheet workbooks and want every prompt to land in a reusable Artifact

Claude Pro with Artifacts is the top AI-spreadsheet pick when long-context accuracy is the priority - the Pro tier at $20/mo unlocks Artifacts (a side panel that renders every output as an interactive mini-app), Projects workspaces for grouping related sheets, and 5x usage. Claude's accuracy on large workbooks (200+ rows, 30+ columns) consistently leads third-party benchmarks because the 1M-token context window (Enterprise) holds entire workbooks in mind. The Team tier at $30/mo per user adds admin tools. The right pick when you are cleaning or analyzing a multi-sheet workbook that no other model can hold in context at once.

Bricks (AI Spreadsheet Builder)

Price: Free tier (limited AI prompts, 100 rows); Pro $10/user/mo (unlimited AI, 10,000 rows, charts and dashboards); Team $25/user/mo (collaboration, shared dashboards); Enterprise custom pricing · Best for: Non-technical users who want AI to build dashboards and visual reports directly on top of an existing CSV or spreadsheet

Bricks is the strongest dedicated AI-spreadsheet dashboard builder in 2026 - upload any CSV and the model returns an auto-generated dashboard with charts, KPIs, and a written summary. The Pro tier at $10/user/mo unlocks unlimited AI prompts, 10,000 rows per file, and dashboard sharing; Team at $25/user/mo adds collaboration. Bricks does not replace Excel's formula engine - it sits on top of your data and produces visual reports in seconds. The right pick when the deliverable is a dashboard or one-pager, not a workbook of formulas.

Who should (and shouldn't) buy

AI spreadsheet tools hit a sweet spot for three buyer profiles and a poor fit for two. The strong fits are: (a) enterprise and SMB teams on Microsoft 365 who want AI formulas native to Excel without leaving the workbook, (b) analysts who handle messy real-world data (CSV exports, scraped tables, PDF extracts) and want AI to clean and re-export XLSX end-to-end, and (c) non-technical users who want AI to build dashboards and visual one-pagers directly on top of their data.

The weak fits are: (a) anyone who only needs a formula occasionally - the free tier inside Google Workspace or Formula Bot's free tier covers most needs at zero cost, and (b) anyone doing complex financial modeling where every formula is custom - AI helps with the boilerplate but you still own the model.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

The category has four pricing models: free tier inside Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace (Copilot in Excel limited prompts, Gemini inside Sheets), per-user add-on (Google AI Plus $9.99, Copilot Pro $20, Copilot for Microsoft 365 $30, ChatGPT Team $30, Claude Team $30), per-user subscription (ChatGPT Plus $20, Claude Pro $20), and low-cost annual subscription (Formula Bot $8.33/mo annual, Bricks Pro $10/user/mo).

Watch for the quota cliffs: Copilot in Excel free tier only ships a handful of prompts, ChatGPT Plus limits uploads to 3 files per chat, and Claude Pro limits daily long messages to roughly 5-10. The enterprise Copilot tier has a 300-seat minimum that blocks small teams - SMB teams should plan around Google AI Plus or ChatGPT Team instead.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

If you only need a formula occasionally, start with the free tier inside Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, or Formula Bot's free tier. If you spend 3+ hours per week cleaning, summarizing, or charting spreadsheets, Copilot in Excel at $30/user/mo (enterprise) or Google AI Plus at $9.99/user/mo is the right upgrade. ChatGPT Plus at $20/mo is the right upgrade for analysts with messy CSV inputs. Bricks at $10/user/mo is the right upgrade when the deliverable is dashboards and visual one-pagers.
