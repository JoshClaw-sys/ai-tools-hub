Who should buy what

The right AI lead scoring tool depends on your team size, budget, and CRM. Here's how to pick:

  • Sub-500-rep B2B teams already on HubSpot CRM - start with HubSpot Breeze AI Lead Scoring ($890/mo Marketing Hub Professional). Breeze AI trains on your own closed-won/lost history. The free CRM tier is a usable starting point for proof-of-concept.
  • Enterprise revenue teams ($30k+ budget, 50+ reps) - 6sense is the right pick if you need intent data + AI scoring + ABM in one platform. The Team tier at $7,200/yr per seat covers most enterprise revenue teams; budget 6-8 weeks for onboarding.
  • Salesforce-centric enterprises - Salesforce Einstein Lead Scoring is the most-trusted option in 2026. Einstein 1 Sales at $165/user/mo bundles Lead Scoring + Forecasting + Conversation Intelligence.
  • B2B SaaS companies with 1k-50k leads/mo - MadKudu at $1,500/mo Essentials or $3,500/mo Growth is the focused scoring specialist that bolts onto HubSpot, Marketo, or Pardot.
  • Teams under 50 sales reps with $25k/seat/year budget - skip ZoomInfo Lead Scoring. The $24,995/yr per seat floor is too high for small teams; you will not see ROI until you have at least 50 reps.

The biggest pitfall in 2026 is buying a $25k/seat/year enterprise platform when your team is only 20 reps - the math never works. Match the tool to your team size first, then optimize for AI model transparency and data freshness. Commit to paid plans only after a 60-day pilot against your own closed-won/lost history.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI lead scoring tool in 2026?

For most mid-market B2B teams, HubSpot Breeze AI Lead Scoring (free CRM, $890/mo Marketing Hub Professional) is the best pick - scoring lives inside the CRM, Breeze AI trains on your own closed deals, and the free CRM tier is genuinely usable. For enterprise teams that need intent data + AI scoring + ABM in one platform, 6sense ($7,200/yr per seat) is the right choice. For Salesforce-centric enterprises, Salesforce Einstein Lead Scoring ($165/user/mo bundled) is the most trusted option.

Are free AI lead scoring tools worth using in 2026?

HubSpot's free CRM tier ships basic lead scoring with manual rules - useful for teams under 1,000 contacts but limited to rule-based scoring, not AI predictive. If you want AI predictive scoring for free, HubSpot's free tier is the only credible option in 2026. Most other vendors (6sense, MadKudu, ZoomInfo, Salesforce Einstein) require paid plans starting at $890/mo or $7,200/yr per seat.

How do you test AI lead scoring tools?

Each tool is evaluated on four axes: (1) AI scoring accuracy on your own closed-won/lost history - measure conversion lift over your current rule-based scoring; (2) model transparency - can admins inspect feature weights and override them; (3) data freshness - how often does the score refresh (weekly, daily, real-time); (4) sales handoff - does the scored lead auto-route to the right rep or workflow. We recommend a 60-day pilot against your own pipeline data before committing to annual contracts.

How much does AI lead scoring cost in 2026?

AI lead scoring ranges from free (HubSpot CRM with manual rules) to $24,995/yr per seat (ZoomInfo Sales + Marketing bundle). Mid-range picks: MadKudu Essentials $1,500/mo, Salesforce Einstein $50-$165/user/mo, 6sense Team $7,200/yr per seat, HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional $890/mo (includes Breeze AI predictive scoring). The floor for usable AI predictive scoring is around $890/mo (HubSpot Pro) or $1,500/mo (MadKudu Essentials).

Do AI lead scoring tools work for B2C companies?

Most AI lead scoring tools in 2026 are designed for B2B use cases with long sales cycles and account-based selling. The five tools we ranked here are all B2B-focused. For B2C companies, look at predictive CLV tools like Optimove, Pecan, or Vidora instead - they score customers (not leads) and predict lifetime value rather than sales-readiness.
