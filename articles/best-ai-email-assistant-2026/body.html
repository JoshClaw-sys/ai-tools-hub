Who should (and shouldn't) buy

AI email assistants hit a sweet spot for three buyer profiles and a poor fit for two. The strong fits are: (a) professionals handling 30+ emails per day who need AI triage and reply drafts at scale, (b) sales teams writing 20+ cold emails per day who need real-time scoring and coaching, and (c) Gmail power users who want AI search and bundled scheduling without abandoning their existing client.

The weak fits are: (a) anyone handling fewer than 10 emails per day - the free tiers on Shortwave or SaneBox's 14-day trial cover most needs at zero cost, and (b) Apple Mail loyalists who don't want to switch clients - SaneBox is the only mid-priced option with Apple Mail compatibility at $7/mo.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

The category has four pricing models: free tier (Shortwave, Lavender's 5/mo), annual subscription (Superhuman $30/mo, Shortwave $8.83/mo, Lavender $29/mo, SaneBox $7/mo), trial only with paid-only entry (Superhuman, SaneBox), and one-off licenses (rare in this category). Watch for the pricing cliffs: SaneBox scales from $7/mo to $36/mo across three tiers, Lavender scales from $29/mo to $58/mo, and Mailmeteor scales from $49/mo to $99/mo. The annual plan saves 15-20% on Superhuman and Shortwave.

Free trials vary. Superhuman ships a 7-day trial, SaneBox ships a 14-day trial (longest in the category), Shortwave ships a permanent free tier with basic search, and Lavender ships a permanent free tier with 5 analyses per month. EmailTriager and Mailmeteor ship 7-day trials.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

If you handle 10-30 emails per day, start with Shortwave's free tier or SaneBox's 14-day trial. If you handle 30+ emails per day, Superhuman's $30/mo annual is the strongest long-term value. Upgrade to Lavender if you run a sales team (20+ cold emails/day). Skip Mailmeteor unless you run cold-email campaigns as your primary workflow.
