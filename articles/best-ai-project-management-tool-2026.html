Tools were scored on five dimensions:

  • AI depth -- summarization, content generation, action-taking AI, and natural-language automations.

The products we recommend

⭐ Top Pick

1. ClickUp Brain

Free tier; Unlimited $7/user/mo + Brain $5/user/mo; Business $12/user/mo + Brain $7/user/mo; Enterprise custom

Best for: Cross-functional teams of 5-50 that want one workspace for tasks, docs, whiteboards, chat, and AI -- with the deepest integration library in the category.

ClickUp Brain is the AI layer ClickUp shipped in 2023 and aggressively expanded through 2026. It runs across every ClickUp surface -- tasks, docs, comments, chat, whiteboards -- and handles summarization, AI writing, subtask generation, standup drafting, and natural-language automations. The 2026 release added AI Agents that can take actions (assign tasks, update statuses, post standups) on triggers. G2 ranks ClickUp the leader in Project Management for the third consecutive quarter in 2026.

Key specs: Free tier with limited AI credits, Unlimited + Brain at $12/user/mo, Business + Brain at $19/user/mo, Enterprise custom. AI Agents run triggered automations on tasks and chats. Native integrations with Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Figma, Zoom, Salesforce, and HubSpot (50+). Mobile and desktop apps on all platforms.

Pros: Lowest end-to-end AI PM price at $12/user/mo for the Unlimited+Brain bundle. Broadest feature set -- docs, whiteboards, goals, chat, time tracking all in one. AI Agents are the most capable action-taking AI in any PM tool in 2026.

Cons: Interface density is high -- new users need 1-2 weeks to learn the navigation. AI credit metering can be confusing -- track usage at the team level. Mobile app is functional but lags desktop on advanced views.

✅ Runner-up

2. Motion (AI Scheduler + Projects)

Individual $19/mo; Team $29/user/mo (annual); Enterprise custom pricing

Best for: Solo founders, consultants, and small teams (under 10 people) that want an AI scheduler that auto-builds calendars from tasks and deadlines.

Motion's 2026 release kept what made it famous -- the auto-scheduling AI that slots tasks into your calendar based on priority, deadlines, and existing meetings -- and added team Projects that pull the same scheduling intelligence across shared task lists. The AI also flags overcommitment before it happens. Motion is the cleanest AI-first PM experience in 2026, but it is not a full project management platform -- it is best paired with a doc tool (Notion, Google Docs) for documentation.

Key specs: Individual at $19/mo for personal AI scheduling, Team at $29/user/mo for shared projects, Enterprise custom. AI auto-scheduling slots tasks around meetings, deadlines, and priority. Overcommitment detection warns before you overload your week. Integrations with Google Calendar, Outlook, Zoom, Slack, Notion, and Zapier.

Pros: Best-in-class AI scheduler -- saves 3-5 hours/week for solo founders. Cleanest UX in the category -- new users productive in under an hour. Real overcommitment warnings -- the AI refuses to let you double-book.

Cons: Not a full PM platform -- docs, dependencies, and reporting are limited. Pricier than ClickUp at $29/user/mo for Team tier. Team features are newer -- some reporting gaps remain in 2026.

💼 Enterprise Pick

3. Monday.com AI (AI Blocks + Sidekick)

Basic $12/seat/mo; Standard $14/seat/mo; Pro $22/seat/mo; Enterprise custom; AI Blocks and Sidekick require Pro tier or above

Best for: Mid-market and enterprise teams that want the deepest dashboards and reporting in the category, with AI as a layer on top.

Monday.com shipped two AI features in 2026: AI Blocks (recipe-style AI actions that run in workflows) and Sidekick (a conversational AI assistant that answers questions about your boards and projects). Monday's underlying platform is the most customizable in the category -- you can build any workflow -- and the 2026 AI layer assumes you have a complex board setup that benefits from natural-language querying. Best for teams that already run Monday and need more reporting depth.

Key specs: Basic at $12/seat/mo for core boards, Pro at $22/seat/mo with AI Blocks and Sidekick, Enterprise custom. AI Blocks are recipe-style AI actions in workflows. Sidekick is a conversational AI for board queries and insights. Integrations with 200+ tools including Slack, Jira, Salesforce, and GitHub.

Pros: Deepest dashboards and reporting in the category -- enterprise teams love it. Most customizable boards -- you can model any workflow. AI Blocks let ops teams build AI automations without code.

Cons: AI features require Pro tier ($22/seat/mo) -- expensive at scale. Steepest learning curve in the category for new users. Per-AI-Block metering can be confusing -- track usage monthly.

⚙ Workflow Pick

4. Asana AI Studio

Personal free; Starter $10.99/user/mo; Advanced $24.99/user/mo (includes AI Studio); Enterprise $30/user/mo+

Best for: Teams already on Asana that want to add AI workflow automation and portfolio-level insights without switching platforms.

Asana AI Studio is a 2026 expansion of Asana's AI features -- it bundles smart fields, smart goals, AI status updates, and a new workflow builder that lets you design multi-step AI automations. Asana's underlying PM platform is the most polished in the category for marketing, creative, and operations teams. AI Studio is the right pick if Asana is already your workspace and you want to layer AI on top without migration risk.

Key specs: Personal free tier, Starter at $10.99/user/mo, Advanced with AI Studio at $24.99/user/mo, Enterprise at $30+/user/mo. AI Studio features include smart fields, smart goals, AI status, and a workflow builder. Integrations with 200+ tools including Slack, Google Drive, Adobe Creative Cloud, and Zoom.

Pros: Most polished UX in the category for marketing/creative/ops teams. AI Studio is a clean upgrade path for existing Asana users. Portfolio-level AI insights work well for agencies and consultancies.

Cons: AI Studio is only on Advanced tier ($24.99/user/mo) -- pricey. Less customizable than Monday.com for complex workflows. Reporting depth is shallower than Monday for enterprise dashboards.

📄 Docs-First Pick

5. Notion AI (Project Management + Docs)

Free limited AI; Plus $10/user/mo with AI add-on; Business $18/user/mo with AI included; Enterprise custom

Best for: Teams that live in docs and want a flexible PM system built on Notion databases -- with AI writing and summarization built in.

Notion is a database-backed workspace, and Notion AI in 2026 is the most natural AI for teams that build PM systems on top of Notion databases. AI actions run inside any database row, page, or doc -- summarize meeting notes, generate project briefs, extract action items, translate content. Notion is less structured out-of-the-box than ClickUp or Asana; you build your own PM framework. Best for teams that want maximum flexibility and prefer docs-first work.

Key specs: Free with limited AI blocks, Plus with AI add-on at $10/user/mo for unlimited individual AI, Business with AI at $18/user/mo for team AI, Enterprise custom. AI actions include summarize, translate, extract, generate, and autofill database properties. Integrations with Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Figma, and Zapier.

Pros: Most flexible -- you can build any PM framework on Notion databases. AI is deeply integrated -- works in pages, docs, and database properties. Docs-first approach suits content, research, and product teams.

Cons: Less structured than ClickUp or Asana -- teams need 2-4 weeks to build their PM system. AI credit metering on Free tier is tight -- teams hit limits fast. Reporting depth is shallow -- dashboards require manual setup.

❌ Skip

6. Wrike (AI + Work Intelligence)

Team $9/user/mo; Business $24.80/user/mo; Enterprise custom; AI features require Business tier or above

Best for: Skip in 2026 -- Wrike's AI features are shallower than ClickUp, Asana, or Monday, and pricing has crept up.

Wrike ships AI features in 2026 -- Work Intelligence for risk prediction, AI content generation, and project summaries -- but the AI depth is shallower than the leading tools. Pricing has crept up (Business is $24.80/user/mo) and the AI features are gated to Business tier or above. Wrike is still a solid PM platform for marketing teams that need proofing and approvals, but it is not the best AI PM choice in 2026.

Key specs: Team at $9/user/mo with no AI, Business at $24.80/user/mo with Work Intelligence and AI features, Enterprise custom. Work Intelligence covers risk prediction, AI summaries, and content generation. Integrations with 400+ tools including Salesforce, Adobe, Slack, Jira.

Pros: Solid proofing and approval workflows for marketing/creative teams. Wide integration library. Enterprise security and compliance.

Cons: AI features require Business tier ($24.80/user/mo) -- pricey. AI depth is shallower than ClickUp Brain or Asana AI Studio. UX density is high -- new users need 1-2 weeks to ramp up. Pace of AI innovation has slowed in 2026.

Who should pick what

  • Solo founders and consultants -- Motion at $19/mo. The AI scheduler alone saves 3-5 hours/week, and you do not need a full PM platform for a one-person operation.
  • Cross-functional teams of 5-50 -- ClickUp Brain at $12/user/mo (Unlimited + Brain). One workspace for tasks, docs, whiteboards, chat, and AI is hard to beat on price.
  • Existing Asana teams -- Asana AI Studio at $24.99/user/mo (Advanced). Avoids migration cost and adds smart fields, smart goals, and AI status updates.
  • Mid-market and enterprise -- Monday.com AI on the Pro tier ($22/seat/mo). Deepest dashboards, AI Blocks for ops automation, Sidekick for natural-language board queries.
  • Docs-first teams -- Notion AI at $10/user/mo (Plus with AI add-on). Maximum flexibility if you are willing to build your own PM framework.
  • Avoid in 2026 -- Wrike. AI features are gated to the Business tier ($24.80/user/mo) and the AI depth is shallower than ClickUp Brain or Asana AI Studio.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI project management tool in 2026?

For most teams, ClickUp Brain is the strongest AI project management tool in 2026 because it bundles AI writing, summarization, and task automation into one workspace at $9/mo per user. For solo founders and small teams that want auto-scheduling first, Motion is the better pick. For enterprise reporting and dashboards, Monday.com AI still leads.

How much does AI project management software cost in 2026?

Sticker prices range from free tiers (ClickUp Free, Asana Personal, Trello) to $29/user/mo (Asana AI Studio). Most 2026 plans also charge for AI usage as separate credits -- ClickUp Brain uses monthly AI credits, Asana AI Studio caps AI actions per plan, Monday AI Blocks charge per Block used. A 10-person team should budget $150-400/mo total for AI features.

Can AI project management tools replace a project manager?

AI tools automate the routine parts of project management -- status updates, meeting summaries, subtask generation, risk flagging, and dependency tracking. They do not replace the planning, prioritization, stakeholder management, and conflict resolution that human PMs do. The best 2026 teams use AI to free up 5-10 hours per PM per week on admin, not to eliminate the role.

Is ClickUp Brain better than Asana AI Studio?

Different shapes. ClickUp Brain is bundled into ClickUp's $9/mo Unlimited tier and works across docs, tasks, comments, and chat -- cheaper and broader. Asana AI Studio is a separate add-on to Asana Business ($24.99/user/mo) focused on workflow automation and portfolio-level AI insights. If you already run Asana, AI Studio is the path of least resistance; if you are starting fresh, ClickUp is the better default.

What is the cheapest AI project management tool?

ClickUp Free with the Brain add-on trial is the cheapest usable option -- $0 base, then $5/user/mo for the Brain add-on. Below that price point, the AI features are limited to basic text generation. For a true free AI PM tool, Notion AI's free tier (limited AI blocks) or Trello with Butler automation (no AI) work but cap AI capability hard.
