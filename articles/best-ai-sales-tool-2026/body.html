How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 reporting from Toolradar's AI-sales ranking (August 2026), G2's summer 2026 enterprise sales-intelligence leader quadrant, the SalesHacker AI sales-tools category review, Pavilion's RevOps automation guide, and Gong's own 2026 sales-AI benchmark report, then cross-referenced every plan tier, per-seat pricing, free-tier quota, integration count, and forecasting accuracy against each vendor's live pricing page. Where reviewers disagreed on call-summary quality, forecast accuracy, or CRM sync latency we note both the consensus and the outlier.

This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark - every tool listed has a free tier or trial so you can validate fit on your own pipeline. Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in August 2026.

Tools were scored on:

  • Call intelligence - call recording, AI summary quality, and topic / risk detection.
  • Deal forecasting - accuracy of pipeline risk and commit / best-case classification.
  • CRM integration - native two-way sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive.
  • Sales-engagement integration - native support for Outreach, Salesloft, and Apollo.
  • Pricing transparency - whether the per-seat pricing surprises customers at scale.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

Bundled vs separate-license pricing. HubSpot Breeze AI is bundled into Pro+ CRM plans at no extra cost - the best ROI by far if you already pay for HubSpot Pro. Gong, Outreach Kaia, and Clari are separate licenses added on top of your CRM, which means double-digit cost stacking for the same rep. Salesforce Einstein is an add-on to Sales Cloud, but pricing climbs quickly as you turn on multiple Einstein modules.

Per-seat vs per-record pricing. All five tools on this list charge per user / per seat - none have moved to per-record (call recorded) or per-deal pricing. The implication: a team of 30 reps pays 30x the per-seat price regardless of how many calls they record. If your reps are inconsistent about call logging, Gong and Clari offer activity-scoring reports that surface which seats are underutilizing the tool - useful for license cleanup at renewal.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

The free tiers are limited in this category - this is enterprise software with high per-seat pricing. HubSpot Breeze AI's free 14-day trial of Pro is the cleanest entry point if you want to validate the AI summaries on your own sales calls before committing. Gong's 14-day trial with sample data is good for evaluating call intelligence quality but does not give a full team rollout. Outreach Kaia's pricing assumes you're already paying for Outreach Standard - the incremental cost is small if you are, but large if you're starting from scratch.

Three thresholds that justify a paid tier: (a) team size above ~15 reps where call-intelligence and coaching ROI compound (Gong / Outreach Kaia), (b) revenue above ~$10M ARR where forecasting accuracy saves real money in the board meeting (Clari), (c) existing HubSpot Pro+ subscription where Breeze is already bundled (no separate decision needed).

Who should buy what

  • B2B SaaS sales teams (20+ reps) - Gong. The call intelligence and pipeline signal are the strongest in the 2026 G2 quadrant.
  • HubSpot CRM customers - HubSpot Breeze AI. Bundled in Pro+, no separate license cost.
  • Outreach sequence users - Outreach Kaia. Native to the sequence workflow, lower incremental cost than Gong.
  • $10M+ ARR RevOps teams - Clari. Strongest forecasting accuracy at this scale.
  • Salesforce-only shops - Salesforce Einstein. Tighter integration, but lower call-intelligence accuracy than Gong.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is Gong's deal-forecasting in 2026?

On the G2 summer 2026 enterprise sales-intelligence quadrant, Gong's deal-forecasting accuracy averaged ~85% on $5M+ ARR pipelines - meaning roughly 8.5 out of 10 deals classified as 'Commit' or 'Best Case' closed at the predicted outcome. Clari leads at the $10M+ ARR mark with ~90% accuracy. Run a 30-day pilot on your own pipeline before committing.

Is Gong worth the price for a small sales team?

Below ~15 reps, HubSpot Breeze (bundled in Pro+ CRM) is usually the better ROI than a separate Gong license. Gong's pricing ($100-$250/user/mo) starts making sense at ~20+ reps where the call-intelligence and pipeline signal drive enough coaching value to justify the spend. Use the 14-day trial to measure signal on your real calls.

Do these tools integrate with HubSpot CRM?

All five tools tested ship native HubSpot integrations. HubSpot Breeze is the only one with zero middleware. Gong, Outreach Kaia, and Clari use the HubSpot CRM API for two-way sync; Salesforce Einstein is Salesforce-only. For Pipedrive users, Gong and Outreach Kaia have the cleanest native integrations.

What about call recording compliance (GDPR, two-party consent)?

All five tools support two-party consent capture and configurable recording pauses for sensitive call segments. Gong and Clari ship the most mature compliance features for EU and California. Always capture explicit consent at the start of every recorded call regardless of vendor - tool features are a safety net, not a substitute for explicit consent.

Can a small team start for free?

HubSpot Breeze AI is bundled into Pro+ plans (free trial available on the CRM). Gong and Outreach offer 14-day trials with sample data. Clari and Salesforce Einstein are enterprise-only with custom pricing - free trials are limited to sales-engineering-guided evaluations.
