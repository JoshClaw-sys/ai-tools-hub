Who should pick what

Categorical recommendations by team profile:

  • Non-technical business teams (marketing, sales, support, HR, ops) — Zapier. The no-code builder is the most accessible in 2026, the 9,000+ integration catalog covers almost every SaaS you'll ever need, and the Professional tier at $19.99/mo is the right starting point.
  • Operations teams with some technical comfort — Make. The visual canvas makes complex branching workflows dramatically easier to debug and hand off than Zapier's linear step interface. Core at $9/mo is the best price-to-capability ratio for moderate volume.
  • Developer-led teams or teams with significant AI pipeline ambitions — n8n. Self-hosting gives full data residency, native LangChain-style AI nodes are the deepest in any visual platform, and per-execution pricing is substantially cheaper than per-task or per-operation models on complex workflows.
  • Enterprise with legacy desktop systems (manufacturing ERP, insurance claims, healthcare records, financial back-office) — UiPath. The market-defining RPA platform is uniquely valuable when APIs don't exist for the system you need to automate.
  • Organizations standardized on Microsoft 365 / Azure — Microsoft Power Automate. The Teams + SharePoint + Outlook integration is unmatched, and sensitivity-label enforcement plus Azure AI grounding make it the right call for Microsoft estates.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

AI automation pricing splits into three bands. (1) Entry-level hosted automation (Zapier Professional, Make Core, Power Automate Premium) starts at $10–$30/month for small teams. (2) Developer platforms (n8n self-hosted Community Edition) can run at infrastructure cost only — typically $5–$20/month on a VPS — for technically capable teams. (3) Enterprise RPA platforms (UiPath Standard and Enterprise, Workato) involve annual contracts that scale with robot count and process volume, typically in the five- to six-figure annual range.

A few traps to watch for:

  • Per-task pricing explodes at scale. Zapier's per-task model looks cheap on a 100-task/mo plan but becomes punishing on high-volume triggers (e.g. inbound email processing). Make's per-credit model and n8n's per-execution model both smooth this out.
  • "Free tier" is often crippled. Several platforms cap free tiers at 100 tasks/mo with 2-step workflows only. Make's 1,000 ops/mo and n8n's open-source self-host are the most honest free offerings in 2026.
  • Data residency is rarely free. Zapier, Make, and Power Automate route all data through US-based AWS or Azure regions. If you need EU residency or HIPAA, only n8n self-hosted and UiPath on-premise deliver infrastructure-level control — and both require operational investment.
  • Billing models are not interchangeable. Per-task (Zapier), per-credit (Make), per-execution (n8n), per-user (Power Automate), per-robot (UiPath) — each favors different workload shapes. Run a cost projection on your actual workflow shape, not a synthetic example.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

The short version: stay on the free tier while you validate the use case — one workflow, one team, one integration. Upgrade to a paid tier when the workflow becomes part of someone's daily work, when you've outgrown the free-tier task cap, or when you need premium integrations and governance (SSO, audit logs, conditional logic) that aren't on the free plan. The $10–$30/mo individual tier is the sweet spot for most builders; team tiers around $69–$299/mo are only worth it once multiple seats are sharing workflows and need admin controls. Enterprise tiers around $10K+/year are only justified when you're coordinating workflows across departments with audit and compliance requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI automation platform in 2026?

For most business teams, Zapier — 9,000+ integrations, no-code builder, Professional at $19.99/mo. For complex branching workflows, Make. For developer-led teams and self-hosting, n8n. For enterprise RPA, UiPath.

How much do AI automation platforms cost?

Entry-level hosted automation starts at $10–$30/month (Zapier Professional, Make Core). Developer platforms can run at infrastructure cost only ($5–$20/month on a VPS) via n8n self-host. Enterprise RPA platforms run five- to six-figure annual contracts.

Do I need to write code to use these platforms?

No — Zapier, Make, Power Automate, Relay.app, Lindy, and Gumloop are visual no-code builders. n8n and Pipedream assume your team can write JavaScript or Python. UiPath and Workato are enterprise platforms that assume IT involvement.

Which platform handles data residency / GDPR best?

n8n self-hosted (Community Edition is free) and UiPath on-premise are the only options with infrastructure-level data residency control. Zapier, Make, and Power Automate route data through US-based AWS or Azure regions with limited residency options.

What's the difference between Zapier and Make?

Zapier has the largest integration catalog (9,000+) and the easiest no-code builder, but charges per task. Make has a more powerful visual canvas for complex multi-branch workflows and charges per operation/credit, which is cheaper at scale. Pick Zapier for speed-to-value, Make for complex logic.
