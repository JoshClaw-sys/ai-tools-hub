Pricing and plan notes that matter

Per-resolution vs per-seat AI. Intercom Fin's per-resolution model ($0.99 standard, $0.49 if you bring your own knowledge base) is the cleanest pricing model we saw in 2026 - you pay nothing for tickets the AI cannot resolve. Zendesk, Freshdesk, and Tidio bundle AI into the per-agent plan, which means you pay whether the AI works or not. For most teams under 5,000 tickets per month, per-resolution wins; above 10,000 tickets per month the math flips and a flat-rate plan often costs less.

The AI add-on tier trap. Zendesk's headline per-agent price ($19/mo Suite Team) does NOT include AI agents - those are an add-on starting at $50/mo for the smallest team. Help Scout's pricing is more honest (AI Drafts + AI Answers included in Standard+ plans) but the AI capability is shallower than what Fin or Zendesk AI deliver. Always read the pricing page footnote, not the headline.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

The free tiers are actually usable for casual use in 2026. Freshdesk's free plan includes 10 AI resolutions/month - enough to deflect a real share of tickets for a team of fewer than 10 agents. Intercom's 14-day trial of Fin is the right starting point if you want to validate resolution accuracy on your knowledge base before committing to per-resolution pricing. Tidio's free Lyro tier covers limited conversations and is a clean entry for small Shopify stores.

Three thresholds that justify a paid tier: (a) deflection rate on a real knowledge base (above 50% means Fin is paying for itself), (b) per-agent cost when AI is bundled (above $30/agent/mo for Zendesk AI means you should compare to Fin's per-resolution model), (c) team size for shared inboxes (above ~10 agents the cost of NOT having a shared inbox exceeds the cost of any tool on this list).

Who should buy what

  • SaaS support teams (500-50,000 tickets/mo) - Intercom Fin. The resolution accuracy and the per-resolution model beat Zendesk AI on cost per resolution at this volume.
  • Teams already paying for Zendesk Suite - Zendesk AI. Don't migrate platforms just for AI; the Suite AI add-on is solid.
  • Shopify / WooCommerce stores - Tidio Lyro. Native product-page ingestion, fastest install, best price.
  • Small teams that need a free tier - Freshdesk Freddy AI. 10 free AI resolutions/month is the strongest free tier tested.
  • Email-first help desks - Help Scout. Skip the AI deflection race and prioritize the cleanest email experience.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is Intercom Fin on real support queues?

On the G2 summer 2026 leader quadrant, Fin averaged ~65% deflection on real SaaS support queues - meaning roughly two out of every three incoming tickets were resolved without a human agent. Accuracy depends on the quality of your help-center content: a thin knowledge base drops Fin's resolution rate noticeably. Run the 14-day free trial on your own knowledge base before committing.

Is per-resolution pricing cheaper than per-seat AI?

For most teams under ~5,000 tickets per month, per-resolution is cheaper than per-seat AI - you pay only when the bot actually resolves something. Above ~10,000 tickets per month the math flips and a flat-rate plan (Zendesk AI bundled, or Tidio Lyro at scale) often wins. Run a 30-day pilot on both pricing models with your real ticket volume before committing.

Do these tools integrate with Shopify and Salesforce?

All five tools tested ship native Shopify integrations. Salesforce integration is mature on Zendesk, Freshdesk, and Help Scout. Intercom Fin uses the Salesforce Service Cloud connector for two-way ticket sync. For HubSpot users, Zendesk and Freshdesk have the cleanest native integrations; Help Scout and Intercom require Zapier for full two-way sync.

What about voice / phone support?

Zendesk (via Zendesk Talk) and Freshdesk (via Freshcaller) ship voice/phone channels natively. Intercom's voice offering is younger and still maturing - good for low-volume use but not yet a contact-center replacement. Tidio and Help Scout do not offer native voice - third-party Twilio integrations required.

Can a small team start for free?

Freshdesk's free plan includes 10 AI resolutions/month - the strongest free-tier AI of any vendor tested. Intercom offers a 14-day free trial of Fin. Tidio offers a free Lyro tier with limited conversations. Help Scout's free plan is email-only and does not include AI features.
