The interesting shift is that the major platforms - Otter, Fireflies, Read AI, tl;dv, Fathom, Granola - have folded AI summaries, action-item extraction, CRM write-back and meeting coaching into the same workflow where you used to just get a transcript, which means the right question is no longer 'which tool transcribes best' but 'which platform gives me the AI workflow I actually need'. This guide ranks six tools worth paying for in 2026, with pricing verified against each vendor's live pricing page in August 2026, plus the structural plan-tier details that decide your real bill.

Fireflies.ai Best for sales teams and CRM

Price: Free / $18/user/mo Pro / $29/user/mo Business / Custom Enterprise · Best for: Sales and customer-success teams that need deep CRM write-back

Fireflies is the meeting assistant that wins on integration depth: it sends structured notes and action items to Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho and Slack with custom field mapping, which is why most sales-led organisations end up here. The 2026 release added AskFred - a conversational layer that lets you query every meeting you've ever had - and a sentiment score that flags calls where the customer tone shifted negative. Less polished than Otter for real-time transcription but stronger on the post-call workflow.

Read AI Best for engagement analytics

Price: Free / $15/user/mo Pro / Custom Enterprise · Best for: Coaching-heavy teams that want per-speaker engagement scores

Useful for managers coaching a sales or CS team, less useful if you only need a transcript and a summary. The 2026 release added Coachable AI that flags missed cues ('you said um 47 times') and a per-meeting influence score that ranks how much each attendee drove the conversation.

Granola Skip unless

Price: Free / $18/user/mo Pro / Custom Business · Best for: Teams that want AI-edited notes, not raw transcripts

Granola takes a different approach: it does NOT produce a verbatim transcript by default. Instead it listens during the call and produces an AI-edited summary in your voice and structure, which most users prefer if they only need notes to act on (not a record). The trade-off: no searchable archive of what was actually said. Skip Granola unless you specifically want notes, not transcripts.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

Two structural details decide your real bill. First, transcription minute caps matter: Otter's Pro tier ($8.33/user/month annual) gives 1,800 minutes/month per user, which is enough for most teams but not enough for call centres. Fireflies Pro ($18/user/month) has unlimited minutes on its core plans. Fathom is the only major tool with truly unlimited minutes at its free tier - that alone is the deciding factor for solo operators. Second, seat minimums are real: Fireflies and Read AI both require a minimum of 3 seats on annual contracts, which can push small teams past $700/year before they add anyone.

If you only need one or two specific jobs (transcription + summary, with no CRM write-back), it is often cheaper to combine Fathom Free for transcripts with a manual summary workflow than to buy Otter or Fireflies at scale. A practical 2026 stack for a 3-person team is Fathom Free + Otter Pro for the lead generator only - about $100/year rather than $540/year.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

Stay on a free tier or trial only while you are evaluating one specific feature or auditing your meeting volume. The moment you need repeatable workflows - CRM write-back, team-wide search across past meetings, multi-language support, or scheduled summary delivery - you need a paid tier. Upgrade past the entry tier when one of three things happens: you need OtterPilot or AskFred, you need per-speaker engagement scoring, or you need clip editing at volume.

What 2026 changed about AI meeting transcription

The biggest shift in the past year is that the meeting assistant stopped being a transcript tool and started being a workflow tool. Otter's OtterPilot joins the call. Fireflies' AskFred queries every meeting you've ever had. Read AI scores engagement per speaker. tl;dv trims long calls into shareable clips. If you are still using a tool that only produces a transcript, you are paying for the 2024 version of this category.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI meeting transcription tool in 2026?

Otter Pro at $8.33/user/month annual billing for most teams. Real-time transcription quality is the best in category, the OtterPilot join-the-call feature is genuinely useful, and one bill covers the recording + summarisation + CRM write-back that most teams need. Fireflies Business at $29/user/month is the right call when CRM write-back depth drives the decision.

Is there a good free AI meeting transcription tool?

Fathom Free is the strongest free option in 2026 with truly unlimited recording minutes, unlimited storage, and full AI summaries. Otter Free has a 300 min/month cap, which most teams exceed inside the first two weeks. tl;dv Free has unlimited recording but caps AI features.

How much does Otter.ai cost in 2026?

Otter has four published tiers: Free (300 min/mo), Pro at $8.33/user/mo on annual billing or $16.99/user/mo monthly, Business at $20/user/mo, and Enterprise custom. The Pro tier unlocks Otter AI Chat, action items and most integrations; Business adds OtterPilot and CRM write-back.

Which meeting AI tool is best for Salesforce?

Fireflies Business at $29/user/month. It supports custom field mapping into Salesforce, including opportunity stage updates, next-step notes and contact-level summaries. Otter Business also writes to Salesforce but with less granular control.

Do AI meeting tools work for languages other than English?

Yes - tl;dv supports 30+ languages and is the strongest multilingual option in 2026. Otter and Fireflies both handle English-first workflows with limited support for Spanish, French, German and a handful of other languages. For non-English primary teams, tl;dv is the safer pick.
