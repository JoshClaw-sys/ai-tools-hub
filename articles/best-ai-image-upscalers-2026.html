2. Magnific AI

$39/mo or $348/yr

The standout for AI-generated art and illustration upscaling. Magnific AI goes beyond enlargement — it interprets the source image and adds detail that matches the style and context. Where Topaz recovers what was there, Magnific invents what should be there.

What stands out: Style-aware upscaling preserves the artistic intent of AI-generated images. Prompt-guided detail control lets you direct the AI to add specific texture (skin pores, fabric weave, etc.). Web-based, no install needed.

🆓 Best Free Option

3. Upscayl

Free (open-source)

The strongest free, open-source AI upscaler in 2026. Upscayl runs locally on Windows, Mac, and Linux, ships multiple AI models for different use cases, and never uploads your images anywhere. No subscription, no usage limits, no tracking.

What stands out: Truly free, no strings attached. Multiple model choices (Real-ESRGAN, Ultra-Mix, etc.) let you optimize for photos vs. illustrations. Batch processing is fast on a decent GPU.

Limits: Detail recovery quality is below Topaz and Magnific for the most demanding use cases. Requires a GPU for reasonable speed. No face-recovery-specific model.

Source: Upscayl official site, open-source repository.

Included with Photoshop subscription ($22.99/mo+)

Photoshop's built-in Super Resolution feature (via Adobe Camera Raw) is a competent upscaler for photographers already in the Adobe ecosystem. It's not class-leading on detail recovery vs. Topaz, but the integration with the rest of the Adobe workflow is unbeatable.

Where it works: You already pay for Photoshop. You need a single workflow that includes RAW processing, masking, and output.

Where it doesn't: If you don't already subscribe to Photoshop, the standalone cost is high. Super Resolution caps at 4x (vs. Topaz's 16x).

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI image upscaler in 2026?

Topaz Gigapixel AI is our 2026 pick for photographers and print professionals. Magnific AI wins for digital artists who want creative detail amplification. Upscayl is the right free pick. Cloud-based options (Let's Enhance, Remini) work for non-technical users who want one-click results.

Can AI image upscalers really add detail that wasn't there?

Yes, but not magic. Trained on millions of image pairs, the best upscalers hallucinate plausible texture — skin pores, fabric weave, hair strands — that matches the surrounding context. The output is not "the original image at higher resolution" but "a plausible high-resolution version that the model believes should exist." For portraits, this works remarkably well. For complex textures (fabric patterns, distant text), it can introduce artifacts.

How much do AI image upscalers cost?

Free: Upscayl (open-source, on-device). Mid-tier: Let's Enhance starts at $9/mo or $72/yr for cloud-based. Premium: Topaz Gigapixel AI is a one-time $99-$299 purchase with free updates. Magnific AI is subscription-based at $39/mo or $348/yr. Pick the model based on your volume and whether you need offline use.

What's the best upscaler for old photos?

Topaz Gigapixel AI's face-recovery model is the strongest for portraits. Remini (mobile-first, $9.99/mo or $79.99/yr) is excellent for one-tap old photo restoration on phone. For batch processing of archive scans, Topaz Photo AI combines upscaling with denoising and sharpening in one workflow.

Are cloud-based AI upscalers safe for sensitive photos?

Check the privacy policy. Reputable services (Topaz, Let's Enhance, Magnific) do not use uploaded images for training and delete them within hours. For sensitive or client-confidential photos, prefer on-device tools (Topaz Gigapixel AI desktop, Upscayl) where the image never leaves your machine.
