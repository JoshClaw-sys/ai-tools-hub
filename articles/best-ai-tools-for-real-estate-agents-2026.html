  • Mobile dialer, call recording, transcription
  • Reporting on agent activity, lead source ROI, conversion velocity

Pros:

  • Best-in-class lead routing -- the rule engine is the deepest in the category
  • AI Action Plans cut the manual drip campaign setup time by ~80%
  • Native integrations with the major real estate lead sources

Cons:

  • Starter tier caps lead volume; most teams need Professional or Enterprise
  • Setup is non-trivial -- a good admin saves you 10+ hours/week
Strong Contender

Who should buy what

Match the stack to your team size and lead volume:

  • If you are a solo agent with a sub-100 lead-per-month volume, start with Follow Up Boss Starter ($49/mo) for CRM and Canva Pro ($15/mo) for marketing. Add Zillow Premier Agent ($20-40/wk per ZIP) once you have a clear ROI target. Total stack ~$200/mo.
  • If you are a small team (3-10 agents), upgrade to Lofty Starter ($399/mo) for the AI assistant + built-in dialer, or stay on Follow Up Boss Professional ($69/mo) if you already have a separate dialer. Add Ylopo Apex ($499 setup + $299/mo) only if you are running >$2M GCI and need AI Voice for after-hours qualification.
  • If you are a brokerage (10+ agents), Follow Up Boss Enterprise ($99/mo) or Lofty Pro/Enterprise ($799-999/mo) covers multi-seat, admin dashboards, and source-attribution reporting. Zillow Premier Agent at $50/wk per ZIP is the right budget for ZIPs with active buyer demand; cut to $20/wk on quieter ZIPs.
  • If you generate most leads from Facebook or Google ads, Ylopo Apex is purpose-built for high-volume paid funnels. The AI Voice component is the real value -- it qualifies leads 24/7 and saves the cost of an ISA (Inside Sales Agent) at $4-6K/mo.
  • If you are a listing-heavy agent (volume seller, new construction), pair your CRM with Canva Pro or Teams ($15-30/user/mo) for listing collateral -- flyers, social posts, market updates. Canva's 25K+ real estate templates cover ~90% of the marketing you will ever produce.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

Real estate CRMs price per seat, per lead, or both. Follow Up Boss Starter caps lead volume on the cheapest tier; most teams need Professional ($69/mo) within a month of signing up. Lofty's pricing is all-in-one (CRM + dialer + IDX site) which is a value for solo agents but a significant commitment. Zillow Premier Agent is per-ZIP per-week, which means an agent covering three ZIPs at $30/wk each is paying $360/mo just for ad placement -- budget accordingly.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

The free tier of every real estate tool here is too limited for active use -- capped lead volume, no AI features, no bulk send. Upgrade to paid when any of these become true: (1) you are dropping leads because the free tier won't route them, (2) you are spending more than 5 hours/week on manual follow-up that an AI action plan could automate, (3) your ad spend exceeds $1K/mo and you need source attribution to know which ZIPs are profitable, or (4) you are running a team and need admin reporting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI tool for real estate agents in 2026?

It depends on the job: Follow Up Boss for CRM and lead routing, Lofty for AI-assisted nurture and a built-in dialer, and Zillow Premier Agent for top-of-portal visibility. Solo agents can run Lofty or Follow Up Boss + Canva for marketing.

How much do AI real estate tools cost per month?

Solo agents typically pay $50-200/mo for CRM (Follow Up Boss Starter at $49, Lofty at $399 for teams). Premier Agent-style ad placements are $20-50/wk per ZIP. High-volume tools (Ylopo Apex, Lofty Enterprise) start at $300-500/mo. Total monthly SaaS for an active solo agent lands at $200-500.

Is Zillow Premier Agent still worth it in 2026?

Yes, for most US buyer-side agents -- Zillow still sends the highest-intent buyer traffic. The ROI calculation has tightened (impressions-to-lead conversion dropped ~25% since 2023) so budget $20-40/wk per ZIP instead of the $50+/wk some teams paid in 2021-2022.

Can I use AI to write listing descriptions?

Yes. Lofty AI Assistant, Canva Magic Write, ChatGPT, and Claude all write serviceable listing descriptions. Always edit for the specific property's tone -- AI descriptions default to generic adjectives ("stunning," "spacious") that buyers skim past. Pair the AI draft with 2-3 property-specific details for the best result.

What is the best CRM for solo real estate agents?

Follow Up Boss Starter at $49/mo is the best value for solo agents -- lead routing, AI action plans, mobile dialer, and 200+ source integrations. Lofty Starter ($399/mo) is better if you also want a built-in IDX website and AI assistant, but the price jump is significant.

Are AI real estate tools safe to use for client communication?

For initial lead nurture and routine follow-up, yes. For contract-stage communication, price negotiation, or anything involving legal disclosures, AI-generated drafts need human review. Most CRMs (Follow Up Boss, Lofty) make this explicit in their terms of service.
