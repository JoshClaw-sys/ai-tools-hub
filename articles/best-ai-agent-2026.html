Who should pick what

Categorical recommendations by team profile:

  • Individuals and small teams who want one agent working this week — Lindy. Pre-built templates for SDR, scheduler, support triage, and meeting follow-ups get you to a running agent in an afternoon, not weeks.
  • Engineering teams who maintain the agent in code — n8n (self-host) or LangGraph. Both give code-level control, version control, and full data residency. n8n's visual builder is friendlier; LangGraph is the deeper framework.
  • Enterprise IT rolling out 50+ agents across compliance-sensitive departments — Airia for governance and red teaming, Salesforce Agentforce for Salesforce shops, Microsoft Copilot Studio for Microsoft 365 estates.
  • When the bottleneck is app connectivity — Zapier AI or Make. Zapier has the broadest catalog; Make has the best visual builder for complex logic.
  • Autonomous multi-step research and synthesis — Manus. The closest to "give it a goal, let it run" without writing framework code.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

AI agent platform pricing splits into three bands. (1) Free tiers from Airia, n8n (self-host), Make, Zapier, Lindy, and Relay.app cover real pilot work — Lindy's 400 tasks/mo and n8n's open-source self-host are the most generous. (2) Paid individual tiers run $15–$50/mo: Manus Pro at $39, Lindy Pro at $49.99, Zapier Starter at $19.99, Make Core at $9. (3) Team tiers cluster around $200–$400/mo: Lindy Business at $299.99, Airia Team at $250, Zapier Team at $69. (4) Enterprise tiers start in the low five figures annually and require custom quotes.

A few traps to watch for:

  • Per-task pricing becomes punishing at scale. Zapier's per-task model looks cheap on a 100-task/mo plan but explodes when you wire it to high-volume triggers. Make's per-credit model and n8n's per-execution model both smooth this out.
  • "Free tier" is not always usable. Several platforms cap free tiers at 100 tasks/mo with 2-step Zaps only. Lindy's 400 tasks/mo and n8n's unlimited self-host are the most honest free offerings in 2026.
  • Data residency is a paid tier on hosted platforms. Zapier and Make route all data through US AWS. If you need EU residency or HIPAA, only n8n self-hosted and UiPath on-premise deliver infrastructure-level control.
  • Governance is not retroactive. If you ship 50 agents on Lindy and later need an audit trail for SOC 2, you'll be migrating. Pick the governance posture before the agent sprawl.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

The short version: stay on the free tier while you validate the use case — one pilot agent, one workflow, one team. Upgrade to a paid tier when the agent becomes part of someone's daily workflow, when you've outgrown the free-tier task cap, or when you need governance features (SSO, audit logs, red teaming) that aren't on the free plan. The $20–$50/mo individual tier is the sweet spot for most builders; team tiers around $250/mo are only worth it once multiple seats are sharing workflows and need governance.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI agent platform in 2026?

For most people, Lindy — fastest path to a working agent with pre-built templates, Pro plan at $49.99/mo. For enterprise governance, Airia. For autonomous multi-step tasks, Manus. For 7,000+ app connectivity, Zapier AI.

How much do AI agent platforms cost?

Free tiers from Airia, n8n, Make, Zapier, Lindy, and Relay.app let you build at $0. Paid individual tiers run $15–50/mo, team tiers $200–400/mo, enterprise tiers start in the low five figures annually.

Do I need to write code to use these platforms?

No — Lindy, Relevance AI, Zapier AI, Make, Relay.app, and Turbotic are visual builders with templates. n8n, LangGraph, and CrewAI assume your team writes code. Airia spans both with code-level escape hatches.

How is an AI agent platform different from an LLM API?

An LLM API (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) gives you raw model access. An agent platform adds memory, tool calling, integrations, triggers, governance, and observability around that model so you can ship a production agent without building all of it yourself.

Which platform is best for enterprise governance?

Airia — agent inventory, red teaming, PII detection, output validation, and a compliance dashboard are built into the platform. For Salesforce shops, Salesforce Agentforce. For Microsoft estates, Microsoft Copilot Studio.
