Quick Verdict

After comparing Clay, Zapier, Make, n8n, Lindy, and Bardeen across data enrichment, integrations, AI-native features, and sales-specific use cases in September 2026, Clay is the best AI workflow automation for sales. It combines 100+ data providers, AI-enriched lists, and multi-step personalized outbound sequences in one tool. Zapier wins if your workflow crosses 6,000+ apps and you need the broadest no-code coverage. n8n is the right pick for technical sales-ops teams that need self-hosting and unlimited workflows on a flat fee.

If you only buy one: Clay Starter at $149/mo for a modern outbound sales stack. Add Zapier Starter at $19.99/mo if you also need cross-app workflows beyond lead generation.

Pricing math: what does an outbound workflow actually cost per lead in 2026?

Vendor sticker prices hide a wide range of per-lead costs once you account for data enrichment credits and task billing. Clay Starter at $149/mo grants 10,000 credits per month; a typical outbound list build (enrich 1,000 leads with email + phone + LinkedIn intent + job-change signals) consumes roughly 2,000-4,000 credits, so $149/mo covers 2-5 enrichment runs per month. Zapier Professional at $49/mo bills 750 tasks per month; a 6-step outbound workflow (enrich, score, route, notify, log, follow-up) consumes 1 task per lead, so 750 tasks means ~125 leads processed per month on Professional. Make Core at $9/mo grants 10,000 operations, where each data lookup or HTTP request counts as 1 operation -- at 6-10 operations per lead, Core covers 1,000-1,500 leads per month. n8n Cloud Pro at $60/mo is the only platform with unlimited workflows and a flat execution-based fee; self-hosted Community is free, with execution costs limited to your infrastructure. Lindy Pro at $49.99/mo bundles 5,000 AI credits -- a single AI-generated personalized first line costs ~3 credits, so Pro covers ~1,500 personalized emails per month. The decision point: Clay wins on data-enrichment depth per dollar; Zapier wins on app breadth per dollar; Make wins on raw task volume per dollar; n8n wins on flat-fee predictability; Lindy wins on AI-generation cost per personalized asset.

Free-tier reality check

Every vendor advertises a free tier, but the practical volume varies by an order of magnitude. Zapier Free grants 100 tasks per month and caps at 5 Zaps -- enough for a one-step demo, not a real workflow. Make Free grants 1,000 operations and unlimited scenarios -- usable for prototypes but tight for production. n8n Self-hosted Community is genuinely unlimited, but you pay in engineering time to set it up and maintain it. Lindy Free grants 400 credits and 1 agent -- enough to test the AI agent builder but not enough for production. Clay has no permanent free tier in 2026; the only path in is the Starter plan. Bardeen's free tier is the most generous for one-off scraping workflows. The pragmatic pattern: use Zapier Free or Make Free for prototyping, then commit to a paid plan once you have a stable workflow worth $20-$150/mo.

Integration and ecosystem notes

Beyond the platform itself, the integration surface matters more than the feature list. Clay connects natively to Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clearbit, Smartlead, Instantly, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Notion, and 100+ other providers via its enrichment API -- this is why Clay wins for outbound specifically. Zapier's 6,000+ app directory is the broadest in the category, and its Tables and Interfaces products mean you can build a full app inside Zapier without external infrastructure. Make's app catalog is smaller (1,500+) but its HTTP module and JSON parsing are stronger than Zapier's, so it pairs well with custom internal APIs. n8n's 400+ node catalog includes the deepest code-native support (JavaScript + Python in the same workflow, native AI agent nodes, vector store nodes). Lindy's catalog is narrower (500+) but every node is AI-native, which removes the "configure AI step" work that other platforms require. Bardeen's catalog is shallow but its browser-scraping node is the most capable of any platform in 2026. For most sales teams, the deciding factor is whether the platform already connects to the CRM, sequencer, and enrichment providers you use -- if it doesn't, every additional connector is a week of work.

Frequently Asked

Which AI workflow tool is best for outbound sales specifically?

Clay is the strongest AI workflow tool for outbound sales in 2026. It bundles 100+ data providers (Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clearbit, etc.), AI-enriched lead lists, Claygent for AI research, and multi-step outbound sequences with personalized first lines. If your SDR team lives in Clay + a sequencer like Smartlead or Instantly, you have the modern 2026 outbound stack.

Can I self-host an AI workflow platform for sales?

Yes -- n8n is the only major workflow platform that supports full self-hosting in 2026. You can run the entire n8n server on your own infrastructure (Docker, Kubernetes, or a single VM) and pay only for execution costs, not per-workflow fees. This is the right pick for teams in regulated industries or anyone uncomfortable with per-task pricing.

How do these platforms differ from a CRM's built-in automation?

HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive all ship with workflow builders, but they are limited to data already inside the CRM. Clay, Zapier, Make, n8n, and Lindy connect to 100-6,000+ external apps and can enrich CRM data with information that lives outside it (LinkedIn intent, job-change signals, technology stack, funding events). For pure CRM-native workflows, the built-in tool is enough -- for anything cross-system, an external platform wins.

Which platform has the lowest learning curve?

Zapier is the easiest workflow platform for non-technical users in 2026. Its single-path editor, 6,000+ app integrations, and extensive template library mean most teams ship their first working workflow in under 30 minutes. Make has a steeper visual-builder learning curve, Clay is steeper still because of its data-table mental model, and n8n requires comfort with JSON and code nodes.

How much do AI workflow platforms cost per month?

Free tiers exist across the category: Zapier Free (100 tasks/mo), Make Free (1,000 operations/mo), n8n Self-hosted Community (free, unlimited), and Lindy Free (400 credits/mo). Paid plans start at Zapier $19.99/mo, Make $9/mo, Clay $149/mo, n8n Cloud $24/mo, and Lindy $49.99/mo. Clay and Lindy sit at the high end because they bundle AI credit costs into the base price.
