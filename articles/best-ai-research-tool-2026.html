Who should buy what

Categorical recommendations based on the workflow stage:

  • General-purpose research — Perplexity is the fastest, most accurate real-time research tool in 2026. The free tier is enough for casual use.
  • Academic literature reviews — Elicit is the only tool purpose-built for systematic reviews. Pay for the Plus tier if you do this work regularly.
  • Science fact-checking — Consensus is best for "does X actually work?" questions. The 20/day free tier is enough for occasional use.
  • Executive-style multi-source reports — ChatGPT Deep Research produces the most polished multi-source reports when you need a single document, not a list of citations.
  • Google Workspace teams — Gemini Deep Research is the only one that exports directly to Google Docs and integrates with Drive.

Pricing and plan notes that matter

Most AI research tools in 2026 are priced around a $20/mo individual tier, but the gap between the free tier and the paid tier is where the real decision happens. A few things to watch for:

  • Perplexity free tier — 5 Pro searches per day is the real limit. Once you hit it, you fall back to standard search. Casual users never notice. Heavy users do.
  • Elicit free tier — useful for exploration, but exports and systematic-review workflows require the $10/mo Basic tier or the $42/mo Plus tier. If you only need occasional paper discovery, the free tier is enough.
  • Consensus free tier — 20 searches per day is enough for occasional fact-checking. The $11.99/mo Premium tier removes the cap and unlocks saved workflows.
  • ChatGPT Plus / Gemini Advanced — Deep Research is bundled into the $20/mo Plus tier and the $22/mo Google AI Advanced tier. You do not pay extra for the research agent specifically.
  • Scite — pricing is opaque for teams and requires a custom quote. The individual Pro tier is around $20/mo.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

The short version: stay on the free tier if you do research casually, less than once a week. Upgrade to a paid tier when you start doing research as part of your daily workflow, or when you hit the free tier cap more than once a week. The $10-$20/mo individual paid tier is the sweet spot for most users — the $42/mo Elicit Plus tier is only worth it if you are running actual systematic reviews as part of your work.
