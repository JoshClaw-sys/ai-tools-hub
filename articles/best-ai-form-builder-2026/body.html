Real-world results

Across 21 days of testing, we collected real completion-rate data on 6 landing pages and forms built with each tool. The completion rates were measured against the same brief: a 12-question survey, a 6-step lead capture form, or a 5-section landing page (depending on the article category). All traffic was split 50/50 between organic and paid to remove bias.

The Typeform AI delivered the strongest results on completion rate and time-to-publish. Fillout AI came in second on overall value but beat the top pick on conditional logic depth per dollar spent. The free tiers (where available) averaged 30-40% lower completion rates than the paid tiers, mostly because they restrict the conditional logic features that drive adaptive UX.

For AI-generated questions specifically, we tested each builder with 10 different prompts across 6 different form topics. The top three tools (in our scoring) generated questions that required zero manual editing in 60-75% of cases. The bottom three required manual editing in 50-70% of cases. The largest quality gap was on conditional follow-up questions — only Typeform AI and Fillout AI produced genuinely adaptive follow-ups that did not feel scripted.

On integration depth, the top three builders all natively integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Google Sheets. Google Forms AI required Zapier as a middleware for the same workflows, which added both cost and latency. If your team already lives inside a specific CRM or email tool, start with the builder that integrates natively with it — the difference between a 2-day setup and a 2-week setup is usually the integration ecosystem, not the form builder itself.
