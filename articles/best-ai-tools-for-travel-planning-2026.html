Quick verdict — our top picks

After evaluating the leading AI travel planning tools in 2026, the right pick depends on how you actually plan trips. For most travelers, Mindtrip is the best all-around AI travel planner — chat-based with shared itineraries, map integration, and a clean free tier. For WhatsApp-first travelers, Layla (formerly BeautifulTrip) is the right pick. For backpackers who want detailed hour-by-hour itineraries, Wonderplan is the strongest free option.

The category has matured significantly since 2023 when Roam Around went viral as a "ChatGPT wrapper for travel." The AI travel planners we recommend

⭐ Top Pick · All-Around

1. Mindtrip

Free (commission-based booking)

Best for: Most travelers who want chat-based planning with shared itineraries. Mindtrip is the best all-around AI travel planner in 2026.

Mindtrip's killer feature is save-and-share — you build an itinerary by chat, then share it with friends who can edit the same plan. Map integration puts hotels, flights, and activities in one screen. Founded in 2023 by a team out of Mondee (an established travel industry player), Mindtrip closed a $60M Series B in 2025 and is the most well-funded dedicated AI travel planner.

The free tier is genuinely useful — not a crippled preview. Mindtrip monetizes through OTA commissions when you book, plus a B2B API for travel agencies. The premium tier is still rolling out in 2026; most users will not need to pay anything.

Source: youngju.dev 2026 AI travel planning deep dive

🥈 Multi-Channel Leader

2. Layla

Free (commission-based) · Premium subscription available

Best for: Travelers who plan via WhatsApp, iOS, or web interchangeably. Layla (formerly BeautifulTrip) is the only AI travel planner in 2026 with native WhatsApp integration.

Layla's strength is multi-channel — start a conversation on WhatsApp, continue on iOS, finish booking on web. The single chat interface handles flights, hotels, and itineraries without forcing you to switch apps. Launched in 2023 by a UK-based team, Layla tried a premium "AI Travel Concierge" pivot in 2024 — some features moved behind the paywall, but the free tier remains useful.

For international travelers, Layla's UK-centric content is helpful for European destinations. For US-centric travelers, Mindtrip has deeper inventory.

Source: youngju.dev 2026 AI travel planning deep dive

💰 Best Free · Backpacker

3. Wonderplan

Free with ads · Premium tier available

Best for: Backpackers and travelers who want detailed hour-by-hour itineraries. Wonderplan generates the most detailed multi-day itineraries in the category.

Input a city, dates, and interests, and Wonderplan produces a detailed hour-by-hour plan with specific restaurant recommendations, activity timing, and transit suggestions. The UI is clean and the free tier is generous — most features are unlocked. Popular with backpackers and thirty-something travelers who want to maximize every day of a multi-city trip.

The trade-off: booking integration is lighter than Mindtrip or Layla. Wonderplan focuses on itinerary detail rather than end-to-end booking. Group collaboration features are also limited compared to Mindtrip.

Source: youngju.dev 2026 AI travel planning deep dive

4. Roam Around

Free with ads · Optional commission booking

Best for: Travelers who plan on mobile, city-by-city. Roam Around is mobile-first and great for solo travelers.

Roam Around went viral in 2023 as a "ChatGPT wrapper" for travel and matured through 2024-2025 into a more polished product with deeper city-by-city curation. The mobile experience is the strongest in the category — the web app exists but is less polished. Free tier with ads; optional commission-based booking when you want to reserve.

The trade-off: single-user focused — no group collaboration features. Booking integration is optional rather than core. For solo city trips, Roam Around is the right shape; for group trips with shared editing, Mindtrip is better.

Source: youngju.dev 2026 AI travel planning deep dive

⛔ Skip for Full Planning

5. ChatGPT (as a full trip planner)

Free / $20/mo Plus

Skip if: You need to book flights, share editable itineraries with a group, or use map integration. ChatGPT is great for a first-pass itinerary draft, but dedicated tools win for full trip planning.

ChatGPT cannot book flights or hotels, has no save-and-share features for group trips, no map integration, and limited memory across trips. Use it for the initial "plan me a 5-night Bali itinerary" prompt, then move to Mindtrip or Layla to actually finalize and book.

Who should pick what

  • Solo travelers — Roam Around for city trips, Mindtrip for multi-country itineraries. Both free tiers are usable.
  • Couples — Mindtrip's shared itineraries work well for two people planning together. Layla works if one of you lives on WhatsApp.
  • Families with kids — Mindtrip's map integration and group collaboration shine. Add Wonderplan if you want hour-by-hour kid-friendly activity plans.
  • Group trips (4+ people) — Mindtrip is the only category leader with true shared-editing. Layla and Wonderplan are weaker for this use case.
  • Backpackers and budget travelers — Wonderplan's detailed free itineraries and backpacker-friendly recommendations are the right shape. Add GuideGeek (free WhatsApp bot) for instant "what's good nearby" answers.
  • Business travelers — TripIt remains the gold standard for auto-parsed email itineraries ($49/year Pro). Use TripIt for trip organization and Mindtrip or Layla for leisure planning.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI travel planner in 2026?

For most travelers, Mindtrip is the best all-around AI travel planner in 2026 — chat-based planning with shared itineraries, map integration, and OTA commissions baked in. For WhatsApp-first travelers, Layla (formerly BeautifulTrip) is the right pick. For backpackers who want detailed hour-by-hour itineraries, Wonderplan is the strongest free option. For mobile-first city-by-city curation, Roam Around is the choice.

Are AI travel planners free?

Most dedicated AI travel planners offer generous free tiers in 2026. Mindtrip is free with optional premium features. Layla offers a free tier with commission-based monetization. Wonderplan has a generous free tier with ads. Roam Around is free with ads and optional commission booking. For business travelers who want auto-parsed itineraries from email, TripIt remains the standard (free with Pro at $49/year).

Can AI travel planners book flights and hotels?

Yes — most dedicated AI travel planners in 2026 integrate with OTAs (Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda) and can book directly. Mindtrip and Layla have the deepest booking integrations. Wonderplan focuses on itinerary detail rather than booking. Roam Around and GuideGeek lean toward instant recommendations rather than end-to-end booking.

Should I use a dedicated AI travel planner or ChatGPT?

For a one-off question like 'plan me a 5-night itinerary in Bali', ChatGPT or Gemini will give you a usable draft in 30 seconds. For ongoing trip planning with shareable itineraries, map integration, booking, and group collaboration, a dedicated AI travel planner like Mindtrip is the better tool. The dedicated tools also remember your preferences across trips.

Do AI travel planners work for group trips?

Mindtrip has the best group collaboration features in 2026 — shared itineraries where friends can edit the same plan. Wonderplan and Layla offer limited sharing. Roam Around is single-user focused. For complex group trips with multiple stakeholders, Mindtrip is the right shape.
