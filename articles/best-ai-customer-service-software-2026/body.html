Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI customer service software in 2026?

For most support teams, Intercom Fin is the strongest AI customer service platform in 2026 — per-resolution pricing ($0.99/outcome), fast setup, and the deepest integration library. For e-commerce brands on Shopify, Gorgias is the better pick. For large enterprise support orgs, Zendesk AI still wins on reporting and routing depth.

How much does AI customer service software cost in 2026?

Sticker prices range from free (Tidio) to $89+/agent/mo (Freshdesk Enterprise, Kustomer). But real 2026 cost is dominated by AI usage: Fin charges $0.99 per resolved ticket, Agentforce charges per Flex Credit, Tidio charges $32.50/mo extra for Lyro AI conversations. At 1,000 tickets/mo, expect $50-300/mo for AI alone on top of base seats.

Can AI customer service tools actually resolve tickets without a human?

Yes for tier-1 questions (order status, returns, password resets, basic troubleshooting). Independent benchmarks in 2026 show AI agents resolve 40-65% of tier-1 tickets end-to-end. Anything requiring account judgement, refund approvals, or empathetic handling should still escalate to a human — and the best platforms make that handoff seamless.

Is Intercom Fin better than Zendesk AI?

Different shapes. Intercom Fin is faster to deploy and uses outcome-based pricing ($0.99 per resolved ticket), making it ideal for teams that want to ship AI in days. Zendesk AI is bundled into the Zendesk Suite and shines when you already run Zendesk and need deep routing, macros, and enterprise reporting. If you're starting fresh, Fin is the better default; if you're on Zendesk already, AI is the path of least resistance.

What is the cheapest AI customer service tool?

Tidio with the Lyro AI add-on is the cheapest usable option in 2026 — Starter ($29/mo) + Lyro ($32.50/mo) = ~$60-150/mo depending on conversation volume. The free tier of Tidio works for very small stores but caps AI conversations. Below that price point, quality drops sharply.
