For anyone who wants the logo to look like a designer made it, Brandmark wins.

Midjourney (Logo Mode) Best for unique, illustration-led logos

Price: $10/mo Basic / $30/mo Standard / $60/mo Pro / $120/mo Mega · Best for: Brands that want an illustration-led logo no template tool can produce

Midjourney v7 in 2026 has a dedicated logo style mode that generates illustration-led, high-concept logos no template tool can match - mascot logos, abstract geometric marks, generative pattern logos. The catch is everything is raster at native resolution: you pay Midjourney for the image, then vectorise it in Illustrator or VectorMagic, and you do not get a matching brand kit or a font licence. For brands whose value prop is the unique mark (creative agencies, restaurants, gaming projects), this is the strongest option in 2026. For 'I need a clean sans-serif wordmark by Friday', Midjourney is overkill.

Ideogram Best for typography-led logos

Price: Free / $8/mo Basic / $16/mo Plus / $48/mo Pro · Best for: Brands that need a clean typographic mark with custom letterforms

Ideogram is the 2026 leader for typography-led logos - custom letterforms, monograms and wordmarks where the typography is the design. It handles kerning, ligatures and letter-shape variants better than Midjourney for type-heavy marks. The free tier gives you 25 generations/day which is enough to evaluate, and the Basic plan at $8/mo is the cheapest paid option in this list. Vectorisation and brand kit are still on you, but for a brand whose logo is essentially 'good typography', Ideogram wins over every template tool here.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI logo generator in 2026?

Looka wins for most first-time founders because $20 one-time gives you a vector logo + brand kit + commercial font licence with no recurring subscription. For unique illustration-led logos, Midjourney v7 is the strongest. For typography-led logos, Ideogram wins.

Are free AI logo generators worth using?

Free tiers are useful for evaluating a tool but nearly all watermark the export or limit you to PNG only. Looka's free preview, Ideogram's 25 daily generations and Canva's free tier are the three with usable free output in 2026.

Can I use an AI-generated logo commercially?

Yes, but the licence terms vary. Looka and Brandmark grant full commercial rights with the purchase. Midjourney's commercial use is permitted for paying subscribers but the mark must be vectorised. Always read the licence page for the specific tier you buy.

Do I need a brand kit with my logo?

For a single side project, no. For a business with social profiles, a website and printed materials, yes - the brand kit saves you hours of matching colours and fonts manually. Looka's $96/yr Brand Kit subscription is the strongest option in this list.

Should I use Midjourney or a template tool for my logo?

If your logo needs to be unique, illustration-led or visually complex (mascot, abstract geometric mark), Midjourney v7 wins. If your logo is a clean wordmark, monogram or icon-based mark, a template tool like Looka or Brandmark produces faster, more editable output.

The bottom line

Looka is the best AI logo generator in 2026 for most founders because $20 buys you everything: a vector logo, a matching brand kit, a commercial font licence, and no recurring subscription. If you need something more unique - an illustration-led mark no template can produce, or a typographic experiment where the type IS the design - Midjourney v7 and Ideogram are the strongest options, with the trade-off that you vectorise the output yourself. Canva's logo maker is worth using only if you already pay for Canva for everything else. Tailor Brands is the one to skip unless you specifically want their LLC formation bundle.
