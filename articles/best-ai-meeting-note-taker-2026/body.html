Pricing and plan notes that matter

Meeting note takers all use per-seat pricing, but the practical cap on free tiers varies wildly. Fireflies caps at 800 min/mo (about 16 one-hour meetings); Fathom and tl;dv are unlimited but cap storage. For a 10-person team running 1-2 meetings/day each, budget $150-300/mo for Pro tier coverage.

Two pricing traps to watch: (1) per-minute billing (Fireflies Business) can spike if you have heavy meeting days; (2) per-seat billing with min seat count (Avoma Business = 5-seat minimum) means you pay for seats even if half the team never joins a recorded call.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

Free tiers cover casual solo use well — 300-800 minutes/month is plenty for 1-2 calls per week. The paid upgrade is justified when: (1) you need unlimited recordings across a team, (2) compliance certifications are required (SOC 2 / HIPAA), (3) you want deep CRM integration (Salesforce opportunity sync, HubSpot contact notes), or (4) coaching scorecards drive sales rep performance reviews.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI meeting note taker in 2026?

Fireflies.ai is the best overall — it joins Zoom, Meet, and Teams, transcribes in 70+ languages, and has the deepest free tier. For enterprise-grade recording + compliance, Read AI or Avoma is the safer pick.

Do AI meeting note takers record the meeting automatically?

Yes. The top tools (Fireflies, Otter, Read AI, Avoma) auto-join calendar events and start recording when the call begins. Most require a calendar integration (Google Calendar or Outlook) and a one-time OAuth grant.

Are AI meeting notes private and compliant?

Vendor cloud storage is standard. For SOC 2 / HIPAA compliance, pick Read AI Enterprise, Avoma Business, or Fireflies Enterprise — each publishes a current compliance report. All vendors let you delete recordings; check retention windows before signing.

How accurate are AI meeting transcription tools?

In 2026, top tools hit 92-97% accuracy on clear English with multiple speakers. Accuracy drops on heavy accents, cross-talk, or poor mic setup. Speaker identification is reliable on 2-6 person calls; degrades on larger meetings.

Can AI note takers handle non-English languages?

Yes — Fireflies supports 70+, Otter 30+, Read AI 40+, and Avoma 50+ languages in 2026. Mixed-language meetings (English + Spanish, etc.) work on Fireflies and Avoma; others may auto-switch to the dominant language.

How much do AI meeting note tools cost per user per month?

Free tiers cover 300-800 min/month. Paid plans run $8-30/user/month. Enterprise tiers with SSO, SOC 2, and audit logs run $25-60/user/month. Most charge by seat; a few (Fireflies) charge by minutes consumed.
