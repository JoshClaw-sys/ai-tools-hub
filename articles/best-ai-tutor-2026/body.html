How we tested (methodology)

We aggregated 2026 reporting from Toolradar's AI tutor ranking (August 2026), the Khan Academy Khanmigo 2026 impact report, Airtable's 8-tutor editor review, the AI Rankings June 2026 study-tools comparison, and Notesmakr's AI study-app guide, then cross-referenced every plan tier, per-month price, free-tier quota, subject coverage, and Socratic vs answer-mode behavior against each vendor's live site. Where reviewers disagreed on subject coverage, language support, or onboarding we note both the consensus and the outlier.

This is an editorial roundup, not a hands-on benchmark - every tool listed has a free tier or trial so you can validate fit on your own subject area. Pricing was verified against each vendor's official site in August 2026.

Tools were scored on:

  • Pedagogy - whether the tutor guides (Socratic) or hands over answers (answer-machine).
  • Subject coverage - how wide the subject range is (math, science, humanities, writing, coding).
  • Pricing fairness - whether the free tier is usable, and whether the paid tier is reasonable.
  • Setup speed - how quickly a working tutor is live on a real subject.
  • Grounded accuracy - whether answers cite sources or are pulled from a closed corpus.

Frequently asked questions

Is Khanmigo free for students?

Khanmigo is $4/mo for individual learners, but it is free for US teachers and for students at Khan Academy partner districts. Khan Academy has been expanding free access in 2026 - check the Khan Academy site for current eligibility in your school district. The $4/mo individual price is the lowest among the major AI tutors in 2026.

Does ChatGPT Study Mode replace a human tutor?

For college-level homework, ChatGPT Study Mode is a strong starting point - the Socratic layer nudges you toward the answer instead of handing it over. For K-12 students who need structured curriculum alignment, Khanmigo is the better fit because it ties directly to Khan Academy's lesson library. ChatGPT Study Mode is the right choice if you already pay for ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) and want a study layer at no extra cost.

What is the best AI tutor for math only?

Photomath is the strongest math-only tutor in 2026. The free tier solves algebra, geometry, and trigonometry problems from a photo with step-by-step explanations. Photomath Plus ($9.99/mo) adds interactive tutorials and word-problem breakdowns for harder problems. For math across a wider range of subjects, Khanmigo's Socratic math layer is a better fit than Photomath for grade-9+ material.

Can these tools help with essay writing?

Khanmigo and ChatGPT Study Mode both cover writing - Khanmigo uses Socratic prompts to help you build an outline, while ChatGPT Study Mode gives feedback on drafts. StudyFetch is the strongest option for converting source material into writing prompts. NotebookLM is the right pick for analyzing primary sources for research essays. None of these tools should be used to generate essays to submit as your own work - the right pattern in 2026 is tutor feedback on YOUR draft.

Are AI tutors safe for kids?

Khanmigo is the strongest choice for K-12 because it is built by Khan Academy, uses Socratic questioning, and complies with US student-privacy regulations (FERPA, COPPA). ChatGPT Study Mode has age-gated features for under-13 users. General-purpose AI tutors should always be paired with a parent or teacher reviewing output for younger learners. Always check the vendor's privacy policy before letting a child use an AI tutor unsupervised.
