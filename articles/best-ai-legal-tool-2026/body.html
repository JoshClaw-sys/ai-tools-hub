Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI legal tool in 2026?

For large law firms and in-house teams, Harvey AI is the strongest AI legal tool in 2026 -- it leads on legal research quality, document drafting, and case-law reasoning at $1,000+/user/mo. For solo and small firm lawyers drafting contracts, Spellbook is the better pick at $100/user/mo. For deep legal research on Westlaw-equivalent databases, CoCounsel (Thomson Reuters) or Lexis+ AI is the right answer.

How much does AI legal software cost in 2026?

Legal AI pricing spans three tiers: solo tools like Spellbook start at $100/user/mo (annual), mid-market platforms like CoCounsel range $100-300/user/mo, and enterprise tools like Harvey AI charge $1,000+/user/mo with custom contracts. A solo lawyer can budget $1,200-3,600/year for Spellbook; a 50-attorney firm should budget $50,000-200,000/year for Harvey or CoCounsel.

Is AI legal software accurate enough for actual legal work?

For research, drafting, summarization, and contract review, yes -- with appropriate review. The 2026 Stanford RegLab study found that leading legal AI tools produced accurate case citations 78-92% of the time and summarization accuracy above 90%. For court filings, expert testimony, or novel legal arguments, AI output still requires attorney review -- the tools accelerate the work, they do not replace the lawyer.

Is Harvey AI better than Spellbook?

Different shapes and price points. Harvey AI is an enterprise platform at $1,000+/user/mo that excels at legal research, multi-jurisdictional analysis, and complex drafting -- built for big-law firms and in-house teams at Fortune 500 companies. Spellbook at $100/user/mo is a contract drafting and review tool built for solo and small firm lawyers -- it is the best value at its price point but does not match Harvey's research depth. If you are at a large firm or in-house team, Harvey; if you are a solo or small firm, Spellbook.

What is the cheapest AI legal tool?

Spellbook at $100/user/mo (annual plan) is the cheapest usable AI legal tool for contract drafting and review in 2026.
