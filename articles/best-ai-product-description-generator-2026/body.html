Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI product description generator in 2026?

Jasper is the best overall for ecommerce stores -- Brand Voice keeps tone consistent across 1,000+ SKUs, the Shopify sync writes descriptions straight into your store, and Surfer SEO integration ships content that's already keyword-optimized. For budget shoppers, Copy.ai's $49/mo plan is the strongest value at unlimited output.

Are AI-generated product descriptions bad for SEO?

No -- as long as you edit before publishing. Google's helpful content guidelines penalize spammy auto-generated content, but well-edited descriptions that include real specs, original voice, and customer-relevant keywords rank the same as human-written copy. Always add 1-2 unique sentences per product (your origin story, a sizing note, a usage tip) to avoid the thin-content flag.

Can AI write Amazon listings that comply with A9 and A10?

Yes for the description body, no for the bullet points. The top ecommerce AI tools (Jasper, Copy.ai, Hypotenuse) include Amazon-specific templates with the 5-bullet structure and character limits. They don't auto-handle backend search terms -- you still need to set those manually for indexing.

Do AI product description tools support bulk CSV import?

Jasper, Copy.ai, and Hypotenuse all support bulk workflows: you upload a CSV with product name + features, the tool writes descriptions for each row, and you export back to CSV or push to Shopify. This is the killer feature for stores with 500+ SKUs -- a weekend of work that used to take an agency 6 weeks.

How much do AI product description tools cost per month?

Free tiers exist at Copy.ai (2,000 words/mo) and Rytr (10,000 chars/mo) but are too small for real ecommerce use. Paid plans run $24-129/mo for solo stores and $249-1,000/mo for agency / multi-store seats. Per-description cost drops to $0.05-0.20 on annual plans, vs $5-25 from a human copywriter.

Can ChatGPT write product descriptions without a paid tool?

Technically yes, but you lose ecommerce-specific features: bulk CSV workflows, tone-consistent Brand Voice across SKUs, SEO scoring against SERP competitors, and direct Shopify/WooCommerce publishing. ChatGPT is fine for 5-10 hand-written descriptions; for any scale, a dedicated ecommerce tool is 10x faster and produces better-converting output.
