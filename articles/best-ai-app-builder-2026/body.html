Pricing and plan notes that matter

Metered vs flat pricing. Most AI app and chatbot builders in 2026 charge by usage (credits, tokens, messages, or seats), not by a flat monthly fee. The headline price is the floor - heavy iteration or high message volume pushes the bill higher. Adalo ($36/mo flat) and Mocha are the rare exceptions for predictable budgeting. When evaluating any builder, read the pricing-page footnote, not the headline.

The white-label trap for agencies. If you're an agency deploying chatbots or apps for multiple clients, white-label branding add-on costs matter. Chatbase charges $199/mo for white-label. SiteGPT charges $39/mo for the same feature. Botpress offers full white-label through self-hosting but requires engineering work. This single line item can be the difference between a profitable agency and a money-losing one.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

The free tiers on this list are actually usable for validation in 2026. Botpress free covers 500 messages/mo, Chatbase free covers 100 messages/mo + 1 chatbot, ManyChat free covers 1,000 contacts, and Tidio free covers basic live chat. The free tiers are tight enough that high-engagement sites hit limits fast - expect to upgrade within 60 days if you see real value from the bot.

Three thresholds that justify a paid tier: (a) message volume above 500/mo (Botpress Plus at $89/mo is the cheapest entry that handles production traffic), (b) white-label or custom branding need (SiteGPT's $39/mo add-on is the cheapest path), (c) team collaboration with admin controls (Standard tier or above on any platform).

Frequently asked questions

Which AI app builder is best for a non-coder founder?

Lovable is the best starting point for a non-coder founder in 2026 - the prompt fidelity is the highest we tested, the credit-metered $25/mo Starter tier is transparent about what you get, and the Supabase + Stripe integrations let you ship a working web app in a single afternoon. Adalo is the right alternative if you specifically need a mobile app on the App Store without writing any code, and its flat $36/mo pricing means your bill doesn't spike during heavy iteration.

Are credit-metered AI app builders expensive at scale?

Credit-metered builders (Lovable, Replit, Bolt, v0) cost more than the headline $25/mo tier suggests once you're iterating heavily - the VP0 Journal June 2026 roundup documented cases where a $20 plan ballooned to $60 in heavy months. The token/credit meters are designed to scale with your build size, so the math works out if your iteration pace is steady, but spikes during prototype crunch weeks can double your bill. Adalo at $36/mo flat is the predictable alternative when you want a fixed budget.

Can I export code from these builders?

Lovable has the cleanest GitHub export of any builder tested - your code stays in a real GitHub repo from day one and the export is two-way sync. Bolt.new exports to GitHub as well, though the export path is slightly less smooth. v0's output is copy-paste-ready shadcn/ui components that drop into any Next.js project. Replit keeps code inside the Replit IDE and is harder to fully migrate out. Adalo's published apps do not export to source code - they stay inside Adalo's runtime.

Which AI app builder is best for mobile apps?

Adalo is the best mobile-first option on this list - native iOS, Android, and web publishing from $36/mo flat pricing, with App Store + Play Store submission built in. For AI-prompted mobile apps specifically, Rork and Vibecode (outside our top 5) are worth looking at - they generate React Native or Flutter code from prompts and support mobile-first deployment. If you need a serious production mobile app with native performance, the honest answer is still to hire a Flutter or React Native developer.

Do these builders integrate with Stripe and Supabase?

Lovable and Bolt.new both ship native Supabase and Stripe integrations - you can wire up auth, database, and payments from inside the prompt. Replit ships with built-in Postgres and auth but uses Stripe webhooks rather than a one-click Stripe integration. v0 does not include backend integrations (it's a frontend component tool). Adalo ships its own built-in database and Stripe-like payment add-ons but the ecosystem is smaller than Supabase.

Is there a free way to try AI app builders in 2026?

Yes - nearly every builder on this list has a free tier. Lovable gives you a free trial before requiring a credit card. Bolt.new, Replit, and v0 all have permanent free tiers with usage caps. Google Stitch is entirely free during its Google Labs phase. Adalo offers a free plan with one draft app (no publishing). The cheapest paid entries are Hostinger Horizons at $6.99/mo with hosting bundled, Framer at $10/mo for websites, and Base44 at $16/mo - none of which made our top 5 but all worth knowing.
