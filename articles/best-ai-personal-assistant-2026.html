The four axes we scored every tool on:

  • AI calendar accuracy - does it actually respect deep-work blocks, lunch preferences, and meeting-free days when scheduling?
  • Free-tier usability - is the free tier usable for an individual, or is it a crippled preview that forces an upgrade?

  • Mobile support - does the iOS/Android app work offline, sync reliably, and feel native?

The 2026 release added Reclaim Habits - an AI engine that schedules recurring personal habits (workout, reading, journaling) at the best time of day. Right pick if fragmented calendar time is the bottleneck.

Right pick for distributed teams where meeting scheduling is the bottleneck, especially engineering, design, and product teams that need to protect maker time across the whole org.

Who should buy what

The right AI personal assistant depends on your bottleneck and team size. Here's how to pick:

  • Solopreneurs and freelancers with chaotic calendars - start with Motion ($19/mo personal). The AI Calendar engine is the strongest auto-scheduler in the category. If $19/mo is too steep, try Reclaim AI's free tier or Trevor AI's $7/mo Pro.
  • Knowledge workers whose deep-work time is fragmented - Reclaim AI's free tier protects deep-work blocks and adds the unique Habits feature. The Starter tier at $10/mo unlocks unlimited habits and AI Suggestions.
  • Distributed teams where meeting scheduling is the bottleneck - Clockwise ($11.50/user/mo Teams) finds meeting times that respect everyone's focus blocks. Team analytics are unique in the category.
  • Users who already use Todoist or TickTick - Trevor AI is the simplest AI task planner at $7/mo. It drags-and-drops tasks into calendar slots without the project-management overhead of Motion.
  • Enterprise Microsoft shops - Microsoft 365 Copilot at $30/user/mo (requires E3/E5 floor at $66/user/mo) ships AI into Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and OneNote. The price is high but compliance certifications make it the only option for regulated industries.

The biggest pitfall in 2026 is paying for an enterprise platform when you only need a $7-19/mo individual tool. Match the price to your team size first, then optimize for AI scheduling accuracy. Commit to paid plans only after a 14-day pilot.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI personal assistant in 2026?

For most knowledge workers, Motion ($19/mo personal, $39/user/mo team) is the best pick in 2026 - AI Calendar + AI Projects in one app, the strongest auto-scheduling engine. For protecting deep-work time, Reclaim AI has the best free tier and unique Habits feature. For team coordination, Clockwise is the right pick. For a simple $7/mo AI task planner, Trevor AI works. Microsoft 365 Copilot at $66/user/mo is best only for enterprise Microsoft shops.

Are free AI personal assistants worth using in 2026?

Yes - Reclaim AI's free tier is usable for solopreneurs with 1 calendar, 3 habits, and basic AI scheduling. Clockwise's free tier offers 1 user with 2 AI-scheduled meetings per week. Motion and Trevor do not have free tiers, but both offer 7-14 day free trials of Pro features. Most other vendors in this category require paid plans starting at $10/mo.

How do you test AI personal assistants?

Each tool is evaluated on four axes: (1) AI calendar accuracy - does it actually respect deep-work blocks and meeting-free days; (2) integration breadth - Google Calendar, Outlook, Slack, Zoom, Notion, Asana; (3) free-tier usability - is the free tier actually usable for an individual; (4) team features - shared calendars, focus-time analytics. We recommend a 14-day pilot before committing to annual contracts.

How much does an AI personal assistant cost in 2026?

AI personal assistants range from free (Reclaim AI, Clockwise limited tiers) to $30/user/mo (Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on, requires E3/E5 floor at $66/user/mo). Mid-range picks: Trevor AI Pro $7/mo, Motion Personal $19/mo, Reclaim AI Starter $10/mo, Clockwise Teams $11.50/user/mo, Motion Team $39/user/mo. The floor for usable AI calendar scheduling is $7/mo (Trevor Pro) or free (Reclaim limited).

Do AI personal assistants work with both Google Calendar and Outlook?

Most AI personal assistants in 2026 support both Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar natively: Motion, Reclaim AI, Clockwise, Trevor AI, and Microsoft 365 Copilot. Reclaim AI and Clockwise also support syncing multiple calendars (work + personal) per user, the strongest multi-calendar story. Microsoft 365 Copilot only works with Outlook since it is bundled with Microsoft 365.
