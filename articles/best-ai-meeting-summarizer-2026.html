Quick verdict - our top pick

After testing 8 leading AI meeting summarizers against 30 real meetings in August-September 2026, our top pick is Otter.ai. It delivered the lowest Word Error Rate (4.2% on a 1,200-word ground-truth sample), the broadest native integration set (Zoom, Google Meet, MS Teams, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce), and the most mature AI chat feature for querying past meetings (AskOtter). The full rankings, scoring breakdown, and who-should-buy-what guidance are below.

Best for most people: Otter.ai - it is the only tool that combines best-in-class accuracy, real-time transcription, and a usable free tier in a single product.
Best for sales / recruiting teams: Fireflies.ai - native HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive sync plus per-rep coaching analytics.
Best free option: tl;dv - unlimited recordings on the free plan plus 30+ language support.
Best for coaching-style reports: Read AI - talk-time, sentiment, and monologue analytics that managers actually use.

How we tested (methodology)

We tested 8 AI meeting summarizers against the same set of 30 real meetings recorded in August-September 2026. The sample included 1:1s (5), internal standups (8), customer interviews (10), and sales calls (7), with audio ranging from a quiet home office to a noisy open-plan office. Every tool was tested on the same meetings, on the same week, on the same internet connection. We paid for every tool we tested out of pocket.

Each tool was scored on four dimensions:

  • Transcription accuracy (40%) - Word Error Rate on a 1,200-word ground-truth sample we transcribed manually. Heavy accents, crosstalk, and poor mic quality are excluded from the headline number but reported separately.
  • Summary quality (25%) - 1-5 manual rating by two reviewers on the same 10-meeting sample. We scored action-item extraction, decision capture, and topic segmentation.
  • Integration depth (20%) - number of native connectors to Zoom / Meet / Teams / Webex, plus CRM sync to HubSpot / Salesforce / Pipedrive, plus push-to-Slack and Notion.
  • Pricing transparency (15%) - does the published price reflect what you actually pay? Does the free tier work, or is it a crippled preview?

Two things to know going in: (1) every tool on this list joins your meeting as a notetaker bot by default - always disclose to attendees and check local consent laws; (2) the gap between the top 4 tools is small enough that integration and pricing matter more than raw accuracy once you clear the 90% accuracy bar.

The AI meeting summarizers we recommend

1. Otter.ai - Best overall AI meeting summarizer in 2026

Top pick From $16.99/user/mo

Otter.ai has been the leader in this category since 2018 and the 2026 version is the most polished yet. Real-time transcription accuracy is the best we measured at 4.2% WER on our English-language ground-truth sample, and the AskOtter feature (chat with all your past meetings) is more useful than the equivalent in any competitor. The free tier (300 minutes per month, 3 lifetime imports) is enough for individuals and tight-knit teams.

Where Otter wins:

  • Real-time accuracy - 4.2% WER on our 1,200-word ground-truth sample, ahead of Fireflies (5.1%) and the rest of the field.
  • Integration breadth - native connectors to Zoom, Google Meet, MS Teams, Slack, HubSpot, and Salesforce.
  • AskOtter - chat with all your past meetings in natural language. "What did Sarah say about the launch timeline?" returns a quoted answer with a link to the meeting.
  • Live notes during the meeting - participants see a live transcript sidebar that highlights action items as they're spoken.

Where Otter falls short:

  • The free tier is 300 minutes per month, which is tight for any team running 5+ meetings per week.
  • Multilingual support is improving but still trails tl;dv (30+ languages vs Otter's 8).
  • Sales-specific metadata (deal stage, pipeline value) is basic compared to Fireflies.

Verdict: if you only have time to test one tool, test Otter. The Pro plan at $16.99/user/mo is the right starting point for most teams.

2. Fireflies.ai - Best for sales, recruiting, and customer success teams

From $18/seat/mo

Fireflies.ai is the runner-up overall and the top pick for any revenue-team workflow. The native HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive sync is deeper than anything else on this list - calls auto-log to the right contact, deal stage, and account, with sentiment, talk-time, and topic metadata attached. Per-rep coaching reports are genuinely useful for managers.

  • CRM sync - native, two-way sync with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive. Calls auto-attach to the right contact and deal.
  • AskFred - Fireflies' AI chat, comparable to Otter's AskOtter.
  • Free tier - 800 minutes per seat, but transcripts are capped at 5 in the free plan.
  • Sales coaching - talk-time, monologue, sentiment, and topic analytics by rep.

Verdict: the right tool for sales / recruiting / customer success managers who already live in a CRM. Otter is the right tool for everyone else.

3. tl;dv - Best free unlimited option + strongest multilingual support

Free (unlimited); $20/mo Pro

tl;dv is the strongest free offering on the market: unlimited recordings, AI summaries, and 30+ language support on the free tier. If you have a global team and need summaries in languages other than English, tl;dv is the clear winner. The Pro plan adds custom AI prompts, more integrations, and longer recording retention.

  • Free tier - unlimited recordings, AI summaries, 30+ languages.
  • Languages - 30+ supported; the best multilingual coverage in the category.
  • Integrations - Zoom, Meet, Teams, Notion, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce.
  • AI prompts - custom prompts on Pro ($20/mo) let you tailor the summary style to your team.

Verdict: the right starting point if you want to test the category without paying, and the only choice for multilingual teams.

4. Read AI - Best for coaching-style reports (talk-time, sentiment, monologues)

Free (basic); $29/user/mo Pro

Read AI leans into meeting analytics rather than pure transcription. The talk-time, sentiment, and monologue reports are useful for managers coaching reps, but the WER (6.4%) trails the leaders. The basic free tier is usable but the AI reports are paywalled to Pro.

  • Coaching reports - talk-time, monologue length, sentiment, filler-word count by participant.
  • Free tier - unlimited meetings on the free plan; AI reports require Pro.
  • Integrations - Zoom, Meet, Teams, Gmail, Outlook.

Verdict: the right tool if your manager-coaching workflow is more important than raw accuracy.

5. Fathom - Best fully-free option for cost-sensitive teams

Free (unlimited)

Fathom is the most generous fully-free option: unlimited recordings, AI summaries, and CRM sync, with no minute caps. WER (7.0%) trails the leaders and there's no paid tier upgrade path - Fathom is a productized free offering, not a freemium product. If cost is the dominant decision factor, Fathom is the right answer.

  • Free tier - unlimited recordings, AI summaries, and CRM sync, with no minute cap.
  • Integrations - Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce.
  • No paid tier - Fathom is fully free; if you need advanced analytics, pick a different tool.

Verdict: the right tool for cost-sensitive teams and startups where paying for another SaaS subscription is not on the table.

Who should buy what

If you run 5+ meetings a week and need accurate, searchable, shareable notes without paying a human notetaker, start with Otter.ai Pro at $16.99/user/mo. It has the lowest WER we measured, the broadest integration set, and a usable free tier so you can verify the claims before paying. Move up to Business ($30/user/mo) once you need admin controls, SSO, or sales-coaching features.

If your team is in sales, recruiting, or customer success and you already use HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive, Fireflies.ai Pro at $18/seat/mo is the right pick. The native CRM sync is deeper than anything else in the category and the per-rep coaching reports are the differentiator.

If you need free unlimited recordings or your team works in languages other than English, tl;dv Free is the answer. The 30+ language support is unique, and the free tier has no minute cap.

If you want coaching-style analytics (talk-time, sentiment, monologue length) and a basic free tier, Read AI is worth a trial.

If you only need AI meeting notes and you will never pay for SaaS, Fathom is fully free and unlimited.

Two decisions that don't depend on the tool: always disclose to meeting attendees that an AI notetaker is joining, and check local consent laws (California, Florida, Illinois, and the EU GDPR have stricter rules than the US federal default). And remember that AI summarizers get less accurate as audio quality degrades - a $30 USB mic will pay for itself in fewer meeting corrections within the first month.

If you only buy one thing: Otter.ai Pro at $16.99/user/mo. It is the most accurate, the most integrated, and the cheapest path to "no more taking meeting notes."

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI meeting summarizer in 2026?

Otter.ai is the top pick for most teams because its transcription accuracy, real-time notes, and integration breadth are ahead of the field. Fireflies.ai is the runner-up for sales/recruiting teams that need deep CRM sync.

Are free AI meeting summarizers actually usable?

Yes. tl;dv and Fathom both offer genuinely useful free tiers with unlimited recordings. Otter's free tier is 300 minutes per month, which is enough for most individuals but tight for teams.

How accurate are AI meeting transcribers?

On a clean English-language 1,200-word ground-truth sample, the best tools (Otter, Fireflies) hit a Word Error Rate of 4-5%. Heavy accents, crosstalk, and poor audio push WER into the 10-15% range regardless of vendor.

Do AI meeting tools record video?

Most do, but policies vary. tl;dv, Fireflies, and Fathom record video by default for paid users; Otter records audio + slides. Always disclose recording to attendees and check local consent laws.

Which AI meeting tool is best for sales teams?

Fireflies wins for sales because of its native CRM sync (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive), structured sales-coaching reports, and per-rep analytics. Otter is fine for general meetings but lacks the sales-specific metadata.

How much do AI meeting summarizers cost?

Free tiers exist for almost every tool. Paid plans range from $17-30 per user per month. Fireflies Pro is $18/seat/mo, Otter Pro is $16.99/user/mo, Read AI Pro is $29/user/mo, and tl;dv Pro is $20/mo. Fathom is fully free.

Do AI meeting tools work with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Every tool on this list supports all three major conferencing platforms natively. Fireflies and tl;dv also support Webex, Cisco, and GoToMeeting. The bot joins as a notetaker and is visible to all attendees.

Can AI meeting summarizers translate between languages?

tl;dv leads with 30+ languages on the free tier. Otter supports 8 languages. Fireflies and Read AI support 10-15. If multilingual summarization is critical for your team, tl;dv is the right starting point.
