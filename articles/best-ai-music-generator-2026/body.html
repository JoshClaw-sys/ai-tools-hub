Pricing and plan notes that matter

AI music pricing in 2026 is structured around monthly credits, not flat subscriptions — the price per track varies a lot depending on length, stem export, and commercial rights. A few things worth flagging:

  • Suno free tier — 50 credits/day (~10 songs) is enough for casual use but output is watermarked and non-commercial. The $10/mo Pro tier unlocks commercial rights and WAV export.
  • Udio's Standard tier — 1,200 credits/mo at $10/mo is roughly $0.03 per track, the cheapest per-generation rate in the category.
  • Stable Audio pricing — $12/mo Professional tier is more expensive than Suno/Udio at the same price point, but you get WAV output and royalty-free rights.
  • AIVA's Pro tier ($59/mo) — most expensive entry in the category, but justified if you need MIDI export + stem export + scoring workflows.
  • Beatoven.ai Creator tier ($7/mo) — the lowest-priced paid tier that still grants commercial rights in this category.

Free vs paid: when to upgrade

Stay on the free tier if you generate music casually — fewer than 5-10 tracks per month. Upgrade to a paid tier when you start publishing commercially, hitting the daily/monthly cap more than once a week, or needing WAV/stems for production. The $10/mo Pro tier on either Suno or Udio is the sweet spot for most solo creators — AIVA's $59/mo Pro tier is only worth it if scoring is a significant part of your workflow.

Licensing and copyright — what you actually own

The single most common mistake in this category is generating on the free tier and assuming you can publish commercially. Every major tool (Suno, Udio, Stable Audio, AIVA, Beatoven.ai) requires a paid tier for commercial rights. The free tier is for evaluation only. If you are publishing on YouTube, TikTok, or any monetized platform, a paid plan is non-negotiable — and the platform's content ID system may still flag AI-generated audio on some tracks, so keep records of your generation history.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI music generator in 2026?

Suno v4 is the best general-purpose pick in 2026 — strongest vocals, broadest genre coverage, and a usable free tier. If you need instrumental scoring, pick AIVA. If you need cinematic or ambient, pick Stable Audio 2.0.

Can I use AI-generated music commercially on YouTube or in monetized videos?

Yes — but only on paid plans. Suno Pro ($10/mo) and above grant full commercial rights. Udio Standard ($10/mo) and above also allow commercial use. Stable Audio requires the Professional plan ($12/mo). Free-tier output is for non-commercial evaluation only and may be watermarked.

How much does AI music generation cost per track in 2026?

On paid Pro tiers, effective per-track cost is about $0.02 on Suno, $0.03 on Udio, $0.06 on Stable Audio, and around $0.10 on AIVA. For comparison, a single royalty-free stock track averages $49 per Artlist's 2026 survey of 2,800 video producers — AI music is dramatically cheaper at scale.

Which AI music tool has the best free tier?

Udio's free tier (600 credits/month, ~40 songs) is the most generous. Suno gives 50 credits/day (~10 songs). Stable Audio gives 20 tracks/month. AIVA's free tier is non-commercial only.

Do I need the API, or is the web UI enough?

If you generate less than ~50 tracks per month, the web UI is enough. If you are a YouTube channel or agency producing at scale, Suno's Premier ($30/mo) and Stable Audio's API are worth wiring into your pipeline.
