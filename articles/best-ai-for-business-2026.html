Who should buy what

Pick ChatGPT Team if: you want the highest-quality general-purpose AI for a team that uses a mix of writing, analysis, and creative work, and you want predictable per-seat pricing. ChatGPT Team is the default right answer for most teams in 2026.

Pick Claude for Work if: your team works with long documents (legal, consulting, research, code review), needs 200K context to fit a whole brief in one prompt, or values writing quality above all else. Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Opus 4.5 are the best long-form reasoners available in 2026.

Pick Microsoft Copilot for Microsoft 365 if: your team already pays for Microsoft 365 E3 or E5, and your daily work is in Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams. Copilot reads your emails, calendar, and Teams chats to give grounded answers — no other tool here does this. Skip it if you are not on M365 E3/E5 — the $30/seat/mo add-on does not stack well on top of Business Basic.

Pick Notion AI if: your team lives in Notion and you mostly want Q&A across your workspace plus auto-summarization of docs. At $10/seat/mo as an add-on, it is the cheapest option here, but reasoning quality trails ChatGPT and Claude.

Pick Google Workspace + Gemini if: your team is already on Workspace and Gemini is included in your plan. You get native AI inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Meet with no extra per-seat cost. Reasoning quality is a tier below ChatGPT and Claude, but the integration is unbeatable for Workspace shops.

Skip Jasper unless: you run a marketing agency and need brand-voice consistency across 50+ content pieces per month with approval workflows. For everyone else, ChatGPT plus a style guide prompt does the same job at a quarter of the price.

Decision framework — upgrade thresholds in 2026:

  • 1-4 people: start with ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or Claude Pro ($20/mo). Skip the team tier; per-seat pricing only pays off at 5+ seats.
  • 5-50 people: ChatGPT Team ($25/seat/mo) or Claude for Work Team ($25/seat/mo). Add Notion AI ($10/seat/mo) if the team uses Notion heavily.
  • 50-500 people: Enterprise tier on whichever tool the team adopted first — SSO, SCIM, audit logs, and data residency matter at this scale. Expect sales-led pricing.
  • Microsoft shops at any size: Copilot for Microsoft 365 ($30/seat/mo) is non-negotiable — you already pay for M365, and the native Office integration is worth the add.

Hidden costs to watch:

  • Per-message fees on legacy API pricing — stick to per-seat models in 2026.
  • Overage charges on chat tools with message caps — ChatGPT Team and Claude for Work are uncapped, but free tiers are not.
  • SSO — usually Enterprise tier only, which requires sales contact. Budget 4-6 weeks for procurement.
  • Data residency — EU customers need the Enterprise tier on ChatGPT and Claude to keep data in EU regions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI tool for business in 2026?

For most teams, ChatGPT Team at $25/seat/mo is the best all-around pick — predictable pricing, GPT-5 access, and a shared workspace. For Microsoft shops, Copilot for Microsoft 365 is the only option that lives natively inside Office files.

Are there free AI tools for business worth using?

Free tiers (ChatGPT Free, Claude Free, Gemini) are usable for individuals but lack admin controls, SSO, and shared workspaces. For a team of 5 or more, the per-seat tier pays for itself inside a week of recovered productivity.

How much should a small business budget for AI tools in 2026?

Plan for $20-30/seat/mo for a general-purpose tool (ChatGPT Team or Claude for Work). Add $10/seat/mo for Notion AI if your team uses Notion heavily. Avoid tools over $50/seat/mo unless you have a specific need they uniquely solve.

Is Microsoft Copilot worth $30/seat/mo?

Yes, but only if you are already paying for Microsoft 365 E3 or E5. The value is native integration with Word, Excel, and Outlook — Copilot reads your emails, calendar, and Teams chats to give grounded answers. Standalone, it is overpriced.

Which AI tool handles long documents best?

Claude for Work with its 200K-token context window is the clear winner for long documents — it can ingest an entire 500-page report in one conversation. ChatGPT Team caps at 32K on Team plans (128K on Enterprise).
